Prose draft · 2023-06-12

Thomas and Natalia — the earlier investigation

User-supplied text. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Download text ↓

1 - 2

The sun dipped low in the sky, casting long shadows across Kyiv's bustling streets. It was several years ago, and a palpable tension hung in the air as news of the invasion spread like wildfire. Amidst the chaos, Thomas Harrington stepped out of the United Kingdom embassy, his first day on the job as a British diplomat.

"Excuse me, do you know where I can find some good local food around here?" Thomas asked in English, hoping for a friendly conversation to take his mind off the turmoil engulfing the country.

The woman looked up, her gaze piercing. "You're not from around here, are you?" she replied in heavily accented English.

"Thomas Harrington," he said, extending his hand. "I've just arrived at the embassy."

"Natalia Ivanova," she replied, shaking his hand firmly. "Journalist."

"Ah, a journalist! Are you working on anything interesting?" Thomas inquired, genuinely curious.

Natalia hesitated for a moment before revealing, "Illegal logging and the impact of industrial activities on ecosystems. I have reason to believe there's more to this story than meets the eye."

Thomas furrowed his brow, sensing that Natalia was holding something back. Their chance encounter intrigued him, and he found himself drawn to her passion for uncovering the truth. But he knew better than to pry, especially when it came to sensitive matters.

"Sounds fascinating," Thomas remarked, deciding to tread lightly. "If there's anything I can do to help, please don't hesitate to ask."

"Thank you," Natalia replied, looking at him thoughtfully. "I may take you up on that offer someday."

"Please do," Thomas said with a smile, feeling an unexpected connection forming between them. "And now, back to my original question. Where can I find some good local food?"

"Try the borscht at this café," she suggested, gesturing to the establishment behind her. "It's the best in town."

"Excellent. Thank you, Natalia," Thomas said, his heart lighter than it had been since arriving in Kyiv.

With a nod and a small smile, Natalia returned to her papers as Thomas entered the café. He couldn't shake the feeling that their paths would cross again—soon. And as he sat down to eat, he couldn't help but wonder what other secrets this city held, waiting to be uncovered by those daring enough to seek the truth.

3 - 4

Two days had passed since Thomas' chance encounter with Natalia, and he found himself wandering through the streets of Kyiv, his diplomatic work momentarily set aside. The city's colors were muted, as though shrouded by a veil of secrets – much like the enigmatic journalist herself.

"Thomas!" A voice called out from behind him, and he turned to see Natalia hurrying towards him. "I'm glad I found you."

"Hello, Natalia," Thomas said, adjusting his scarf in the chilly air. "What can I do for you?"

"Let's find somewhere to talk privately," she suggested, her eyes scanning their surroundings warily. They ducked into a quiet corner café, the dim lighting providing some semblance of privacy.

"Alright," Thomas said, taking a seat across from her. "What's this about?"

"Remember the story I mentioned when we met?" Natalia began, her voice low. "The illegal logging and its effects on our ecosystems? I've discovered something more concerning – politicians may be involved in these destructive activities."

"Politicians?" Thomas asked, leaning forward, intrigued.

"Yes," Natalia confirmed, her brow furrowed in frustration. "It seems they're not only turning a blind eye but actually benefiting from it. This could have dire consequences for our country."

"Your love for Ukraine is clear," Thomas observed, impressed by her determination. "But how do you plan to expose them?"

"Normally, I would rely on my resourcefulness and journalistic instincts," Natalia admitted. "However, given the sensitive nature of this case, I could use some help."

"Help?" Thomas echoed, considering her request. "Are you asking me to work with you on this investigation?"

"Consider it an unlikely partnership," Natalia offered, half-smiling. "Our cultural and professional differences might prove to be an asset."

"Very well," Thomas agreed, feeling a mix of excitement and apprehension. "I'll do my best to assist you. But remember, I'm not a professional investigator."

"Neither am I," Natalia replied, her eyes sparkling with determination. "We'll learn together."

Over the coming days, Thomas and Natalia delved into their investigation, sharing resources and exchanging ideas. Their dynamic was unique – a mix of mutual respect and friendly banter, as they navigated the complexities of their search for truth.

"Any luck on your end?" Natalia asked during one of their late-night meetings at the café.

"Surprisingly, yes," Thomas admitted.

"Oh?" Natalia raised an eyebrow in surprise.

Leaning forward, Thomas said, "It's all off the record, of course – I'm trusting you with a lot here." Natalia nodded solemnly, her eyes filled with gratitude.

"I managed to trace some suspicious transactions that might lead us closer to the politicians involved."

"Thank you," she said simply. "This could be just what we need to expose the truth and bring justice to those affected by this injustice."

With newfound vigor, they continued their work, determined to bring light into the darkness of these secrets and lies.

"Good job, Thomas," Natalia praised him, genuine warmth in her voice. "We make quite a team."

"Indeed," Thomas agreed, smiling. "It's curious how well we work together, considering our different backgrounds."

"Sometimes, it's those differences that bring people together," Natalia mused, returning his smile. "Now, let's see where this new information leads us."

As they continued to unravel the web of corruption before them, Thomas found himself increasingly invested in their mission – and in his partnership with Natalia. The unlikely duo faced numerous challenges, yet their resolve only grew stronger. As they delved deeper into the shadows of Kyiv, the stakes became higher, but neither Thomas nor Natalia would be deterred. They were determined to expose the truth, no matter the cost.

5 - 6

As Thomas and Natalia pored over their gathered documents, the dim lamplight cast flickering shadows over their faces. The musty scent of old books and papers filled the cramped office space they had claimed as a base for their investigation within the British embassy. Thomas could see the exhaustion in Natalia's eyes, but also the fiery determination that burned within her.

"Look at this," Natalia said abruptly, handing Thomas some copies of goverment grants. "I found another connection – it seems there's a pattern emerging."

"Indeed," Thomas replied, his eyes scanning the document. He felt the slow burn of excitement building in his chest. "With our combined efforts, we're making progress much faster than either of us could have alone."

Natalia nodded, her gaze never leaving the papers. "It's strange, isn't it? How well we complement each other? I would never have expected this kind of partnership to work."

"Neither did I," Thomas admitted, scratching his chin thoughtfully. His mind raced with the implications of what they had uncovered so far. "But together, we're coming closer to the truth every day."

As they continued to sift through the mountain of evidence before them, neither Thomas nor Natalia noticed the figure lurking in the shadows outside of their makeshift office. Dmitri Orlov, a Russian operative who had been profiting from the illegal logging operations, observed them with cold, calculating eyes. He knew that the duo was getting dangerously close to exposing his involvement in the corruption, and he couldn't let that happen.

"Comrade, I have news," Dmitri whispered into his secure line. "Our journalist friend is proving to be more resourceful than anticipated. She's teamed up with a local diplomat, and together they're uncovering some...unfortunate information."

"Then deal with them, Dmitri," the voice on the other end commanded, its icy tone leaving no room for argument. "You know what is at stake here. You must protect our interests."

"Understood," Dmitri replied, his eyes narrowing as he watched Thomas and Natalia through the window. He was a master of manipulation and deceit, and he had every intention of using those skills to ensure that their investigation came to a swift and decisive end. The hunt was on.

Inside the office, oblivious to the danger lurking just outside, Thomas and Natalia continued to piece together the puzzle they had stumbled upon. As they worked late into the night, their shared sense of purpose and dedication to uncovering the truth only served to strengthen their partnership.

"Thomas, I think we're onto something big," Natalia whispered, her voice hoarse from exhaustion but filled with excitement. "I can feel it – we're going to crack this wide open."

"Let's hope so," Thomas responded, his fingers tapping rhythmically on the table. "But we have to be cautious, Natalia. There's no telling who or what we might be up against."

"Agreed," she said, her eyes meeting his in a moment of understanding. They knew that the road ahead would be fraught with obstacles and danger, but together they were determined to see justice served.

"Whatever comes our way," Thomas vowed, his voice filled with quiet confidence, "we'll face it together."
