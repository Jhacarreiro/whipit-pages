Side Quests/Books/Shadows of Betrayal

Outline · 2023-05-30

The original twelve-chapter outline

User-supplied text. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Act 1 - Introduction: Chapter 1: Thomas Harrington, a seasoned British diplomat with a background in intelligence, is stationed in Kyiv, Ukraine. He leads an unassuming life, attending diplomatic functions and gathering information for the British government. Thomas has a reputation for being efficient and is skilled in gathering intelligence, speaking multiple languages, and negotiating high-stakes situations. His day-to-day life is mundane and predictable as he navigates the world of diplomacy, but he is haunted by past experiences and the moral dilemmas he's faced.

Chapter 2: The invasion of Ukraine begins, throwing Thomas's life into chaos. He receives a mission from his superior, Marie Duval, to investigate the suspicious death of Viktor Dmytrovich, a high-ranking military officer. Thomas learns that Viktor was working on a secret plan that could potentially turn the tide of the war in favor of Ukraine. If Thomas refuses this mission, he risks letting this vital information slip through his fingers, potentially prolonging the war and causing more suffering.

Chapter 3: Thomas initially refuses the mission due to the fear of putting himself in danger in a war-torn country and the guilt of getting involved in a conflict that is not his own. He also struggles with the idea of leaving behind the relatively safe world of diplomacy for the unpredictable and dangerous world of espionage.

Act 2 - Conflict: Chapter 4: Marie Duval confronts Thomas about his refusal. She shares her own experiences and struggles with making difficult decisions in times of crisis. She helps him see that his unique skill set is needed to uncover the truth about Viktor's death and the mysterious plan. This conversation prompts Thomas to reconsider his decision and accept the mission.

Chapter 5: Thomas steps out of his comfort zone as a diplomat and enters the world of espionage in war-torn Ukraine. He ventures into cities ravaged by battle, navigates military checkpoints, and faces the constant threat of violence. The once-familiar landscapes have transformed into a dangerous and unpredictable new world.

Chapter 6: Thomas meets Natalia Ivanova, a resourceful Ukrainian journalist who has been investigating Viktor's death on her own. Together, they form an unlikely partnership as they delve deeper into the case. Thomas also encounters Dmitri Orlov, a cunning and ruthless operative who seems to be working against them, putting their lives in danger at every turn.

Act 3 - Rising Action: Chapter 7: Thomas and Natalia gather crucial information about Viktor's secret plan, but they still need more pieces to complete the puzzle. They travel to various locations, including Lviv, Donetsk, Chernobyl, and Crimea. Along the way, they meet Olena Kovalchuk, a compassionate nurse who provides invaluable assistance, and other characters who either help or hinder their progress.

Chapter 8: Thomas and Natalia face a life-or-death situation when Dmitri Orlov captures them in Donetsk. They are taken to a hidden location where Orlov interrogates them, trying to extract information about their investigation. During this ordeal, Thomas experiences a moment of "death" when he is forced to confront his deepest fears and guilt.

Chapter 9: Thomas and Natalia manage to escape from Orlov's grasp with the help of Olena. In doing so, they uncover a crucial piece of information that leads them to the true identity of the murderer and the nature of Viktor's secret plan. They now have the key to potentially change the course of the war.

Act 4 - Climax and Conclusion: Chapter 10: Thomas is forced to return to the ordinary world of diplomacy as he travels to Moscow, London, Paris, and Washington D.C. to share the information he has gathered with key officials. He must now navigate the delicate political landscape to ensure that Viktor's plan is implemented.

Chapter 11: In the final test of his transformation, Thomas demonstrates mastery of his newfound abilities as he negotiates with world leaders, drawing on all the skills he has honed throughout his journey. He convinces them to support the implementation of Viktor's plan, which has the potential to change the course of the war in Ukraine's favor.

Chapter 12: Thomas returns to Ukraine, where he and Natalia reveal the murderer's identity and motives, serving justice for Viktor's death. The secret plan is implemented, and a sense of hope begins to spread among the Ukrainian people. Thomas's journey has taught him to trust his instincts and persevere in the face of adversity. He has grown both professionally and emotionally, leaving a lasting impact not only on himself but also on those he encountered along the way.
