Character profiles

Thomas Harrington: A sharp-witted and observant British diplomat in his mid-40s, Thomas Harrington has a lean, athletic build and piercing blue eyes that seem to miss nothing. His short-cropped brown hair is always perfectly styled, and his impeccably tailored suits give him an air of confidence and authority. Although he can come across as aloof and distant, he has a dry sense of humor and a softer side that he only shows to those he trusts.

Maria Dubois: A brilliant and adaptable French intelligence operative in her late 30s, Maria Dubois is Thomas' mentor and superior. She has striking green eyes that seem to change color depending on her mood and a sleek, modern hairstyle that frames her angular face. Maria is always impeccably dressed, favoring simple yet elegant clothing that accentuates her athletic figure. Although she can be reserved and distant, she has a razor-sharp wit and a fierce loyalty to those she cares about.

Natalia Ivanova: A fiery and intrepid Ukrainian journalist in her early 30s, Natalia Ivanova is determined to uncover the truth at any cost. She has long, curly dark hair that she wears loose around her shoulders and expressive brown eyes that seem to sparkle with mischief. Natalia favors bold colors and statement pieces in her wardrobe, and she isn't afraid to take risks or push boundaries. Although she can be impulsive and reckless at times, she has a deep sense of compassion and a strong moral compass.

James Franklin: A charismatic and ambitious American diplomat in his late 40s, James Franklin is a consummate politician with a charming smile and a commanding presence. He has short-cropped gray hair that he wears slicked back from his forehead, and his suits are always perfectly tailored to his athletic build. Although he can be charming and persuasive, he has a Machiavellian streak that makes him a dangerous adversary.

Li Xiang: A resourceful and passionate Chinese historian in her early 40s, Li Xiang is an expert on Chinese artifacts and culture. She has jet-black hair that she wears in a sleek bob, and her traditional Chinese clothing reflects her reverence for her country's history and traditions. Although she can be reserved and formal at times, she has a fierce determination to protect her country's heritage and a deep respect for those who share her passion.

Sergei Petrov: A sly and enigmatic Russian intelligence officer in his mid-50s, Sergei Petrov is a master of deception and intrigue. He has silver hair that he wears short and neatly groomed, and his piercing blue eyes seem to hold a wealth of secrets. Sergei favors dark, tailored suits that give him an air of authority and power. Although he can be charming and persuasive when he wants to be, he has a ruthless streak that makes him a formidable opponent.

Dr. Elizabeth Palmer: A renowned British archaeologist in her late 40s, Dr. Palmer is an expert in ancient artifacts and their significance. She has blonde hair that she wears in a no-nonsense bun, and her practical clothing reflects her no-frills approach to her work. Dr. Palmer is dedicated to uncovering the truth, even when it takes her into dangerous territory. Although she can be brusque and impatient at times, she has a deep respect for those who share her passion for history.

Inspector Chen Wei: A diligent and principled Chinese police officer in his mid-40s, Inspector Chen Wei is determined to solve the case of the missing Dragon's Pearl. He has short, jet-black hair that he wears neatly combed to one side, and his pressed uniform gives him an air of authority and professionalism. Although he can be rigid and inflexible at times, he has a deep sense of justice and a commitment to upholding the law.
