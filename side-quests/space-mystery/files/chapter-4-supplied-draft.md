# Chapter 4 — supplied draft

31 January 2024 · 15:20 · Supplied draft

The dim red glow of the command center's emergency lighting bathed Alex's face in ominous shadows as they hunched over a console, eyes darting back and forth between displays. Their heart raced with every breath, sweat beading on their forehead. The once-trusted faces of their crewmates now seemed to leer at them from the edges of their vision.

"Captain Singh is hiding something," Alex muttered under their breath, tapping furiously at the interface. "And Dr. Hernandez... he's in on it too."

"Alex, you've been at this for hours. There's nothing here," said Elena, hovering nearby with concern etched into her features. Alex barely registered her presence.

"Look at this," Alex insisted, pulling up a communication log. "Captain Singh and Dr. Hernandez have been talking more frequently than usual. They're plotting something, I know it."

"Or, you know, they could just be discussing regular ship business," Elena countered. But Alex was already lost in a sea of suspicion and conjecture, unable to hear her voice of reason.

"Remember when Dr. Hernandez left that meeting early?" Alex asked, feverish intensity in their voice. "He claimed he had an important experiment to run, but what if he was really going to meet with Captain Singh?"

"Alex, come on now," Elena sighed. "You're grasping at straws."

"Am I?" Alex shot back, retracing the days leading up to their growing paranoia. The time when Captain Singh had lingered a little longer than necessary in the airlock, her gaze locked on the distant stars; the way Dr. Hernandez had whispered something to the biologist in one of the hallways, only to abruptly stop when they saw Alex approaching—these seemingly innocuous incidents began to take on sinister undertones in their mind.

"Everything adds up!" Alex cried out, desperation creeping into their voice. "Captain Singh's been acting strange, Dr. Hernandez is avoiding me... there's something going on here, Elena."

"Alex," Elena said softly, laying a hand on their shoulder, trying to ground her friend in reality. "You're seeing shadows where there are none. You need to take a step back and breathe."

But Alex was too far gone, the tendrils of fear and paranoia wrapping around their mind like the cold vacuum of space outside the ship. They continued their frenzied search for evidence that would expose the conspiracy they were certain existed, blind to reason and deaf to Elena's pleading.

"Look at this!" Alex exclaimed triumphantly, pointing at a scrambled message on the console. "This has to be it!"

"Alex, that's just a glitch," Elena replied wearily. "Please, let's get some rest and talk about this in the morning."

"Rest?" Alex scoffed, eyes wild and wide. "There's no time for rest. Not when we're all in danger."

Elena's heart pounded in her chest, a rapid staccato mirroring the frantic thoughts swirling in her mind. She watched Alex pace back and forth in their shared quarters, eyes glazed over with feverish intensity. It was as if the very air around them crackled with paranoia, suffocating Elena and tightening its grip on Alex.

"Alex," she whispered, her voice strained with concern. "You need to stop this. You're scaring me."

"Scaring you?" Alex scoffed, their gaze never wavering from the screen displaying encrypted messages. "Don't you see what's happening? The captain and Dr. Hernandez are hiding something. I just need to find out what it is."

Elena bit her lip, torn between her duty to the mission and her loyalty to her friend. She knew that Alex's current state posed a threat to the team's cohesion and morale. Yet, she couldn't shake the feeling that there was something she could do, some secret she could reveal that might bring Alex back from the edge of madness.

"Listen to me," Elena said, determination flooding her voice. "There's something I've been keeping from you, but I need you to promise me that you'll stay calm."

Alex finally looked away from the screen, their eyes filled with curiosity and a glimmer of hope. "What is it?"

"Promise me first," Elena insisted, her hands trembling slightly as she prepared herself to divulge her truth.

"Fine," Alex muttered, impatience lacing their tone. "I promise."

"Okay." Elena took a deep breath, steeling herself for the confession. "A few weeks ago, I overheard Captain Singh and Dr. Hernandez talking about... a secret project. Something they've been working on outside the scope of our mission."

"See!" Alex exclaimed, their eyes widening as they began to pace again. "I knew it! They're plotting something, and we've been kept in the dark."

"Alex, please," Elena said, desperation creeping into her voice. "It could be anything. It doesn't mean they're conspiring against us. I didn't tell you because I didn't want to fuel your suspicions. I know you've been struggling lately, and I thought this would only make it worse."

But Elena could see that her words were falling on deaf ears. The revelation of the secret project ignited a spark within Alex, fanning the flames of their conspiracy theories even further. Their mind raced, connecting dots that weren't there and weaving intricate webs of deceit.

"Thank you for telling me, Elena," Alex whispered, their voice low and dangerous. "Now we need to find out what they're hiding, together."

Elena's heart sank as she realized the magnitude of her mistake. Her attempt to bring Alex back had only pushed them farther away, plunging them deeper into the rabbit hole of obsession.

"Please," she murmured, reaching out to grasp Alex's hand. "Just remember that not everything is as it seems. Whatever is going on, we'll figure it out together. As friends."

But as she looked into Alex's eyes, she couldn't help but fear that their friendship might not be enough to save them from the darkness that threatened to consume them both.

The dim glow of the console screen cast eerie shadows on Alex's face as they hunched over it, their eyes darting back and forth between lines of data. Sweat trickled down Elena's brow as she watched her friend, realizing the severity of their situation.

"Look at this," Alex whispered urgently, pointing at a series of encrypted messages between Captain Singh and Dr. Hernandez. "They're communicating in code. What are they hiding?"

"Alex, it could be normal protocol," Elena replied hesitantly, trying to suppress the panic rising in her chest. "We might just be misunderstanding something."

"Maybe," Alex conceded, their voice wavering. "Or maybe there's something more sinister going on. We can't ignore these signs, Elena. We need to dig deeper."

Elena knew that arguing would only push Alex further into their conspiracy theories, but she was terrified of the consequences if they continued down this path. Swallowing her fear, she decided to support her friend, hoping that together they could find answers without jeopardizing the mission or their lives.

"Alright," she agreed quietly, her heart pounding in her chest. "Let's go through the evidence we have so far and try to make sense of it."

As they pored over the data, Alex grew increasingly animated, connecting seemingly unrelated events to weave an intricate narrative of espionage and deceit. Elena listened intently, her stomach knotting with unease as she realized the depths of Alex's paranoia.

"Captain Singh and Dr. Hernandez have been working together from the start," Alex declared, their voice trembling with conviction. "There must be something valuable on this ship – something they want to keep secret at all costs. We need to find out what it is, Elena. The fate of the mission depends on it."

"Okay, but how do we do that?" Elena asked, her voice quivering. "What if we're caught?"

"Every risk has its rewards," Alex replied, their eyes gleaming with determination. "We'll need to be discreet, but we can't let fear hold us back. If we don't act now, the consequences could be dire."

Elena felt her stomach churn at the thought of betraying the trust of their crewmates, but she couldn't abandon her friend in their time of need. She steeled herself for the daunting task ahead, hoping against hope that they were making the right choice.

"Let's start by accessing the restricted sections of the ship's database," Alex suggested, their fingers flying over the console as they began to hack into the system. "If there's something worth hiding, it'll be in there."

Elena watched her friend work, torn between loyalty and reason, as they embarked on a dangerous journey to uncover the truth – whatever it may be.

The shadows lengthened within the ship's recreation room, casting an eerie glow over the scattered clusters of crew members. Their voices murmured, blending into a low hum against the backdrop of flickering screens and the occasional clink of glassware. Alex sat hunched in one corner, their intense gaze sweeping over the room as they measured each interaction, every subtle exchange for signs of treachery.

"Alex has been acting really strange lately," whispered engineer Maria to her confidant, biologist Samson. "I've seen them lurking around the corridors at odd hours. It's like they're searching for something... or someone."

Samson shifted uneasily in his seat. "I think it might be the stress of the mission getting to them. We should give them some space, let them work through it."

Maria shook her head, her brow deeply furrowed. "I don't know, Samson. It feels like more than that. There's this... paranoia. And those wild theories about Captain Singh and Dr. Hernandez? It's unsettling."

Meanwhile, Elena stood at the edge of the gathering, her fingers tapping rhythmically against her glass. She watched Alex with worry etched on her face, torn between her fear for the mission and her loyalty to her friend.

"Hey, Elena," called out navigator Ian, interrupting her thoughts. "You seem distracted. Everything okay?"

Elena forced a smile onto her face. "Yes, I'm fine. Just... thinking about our next course adjustment."

"Ah, right." Ian nodded, but his eyes held a glint of suspicion. "You've been spending a lot of time with Alex lately. You wouldn't happen to know what's going on with them, would you?"

Elena hesitated, her heart racing. "We're just close friends, Ian. I'm concerned for them, that's all."

"Concerned, huh?" Ian's gaze narrowed. "We're all concerned, Elena. But some of us are starting to wonder if there's something more going on here."

Elena swallowed hard, her mind reeling as she weighed the consequences of revealing the truth. She stared at Alex, their eyes meeting for a brief moment before they looked away, as though sensing her internal struggle.

"Alex is going through a tough time," she admitted quietly, her voice wavering. "But I'm supporting them, and I hope you can too."

Ian considered her words, his expression softening. "I'll do my best, Elena. But don't forget – we have a mission to complete. We need everyone on board, focused, and working together."

"I understand," she replied, nodding solemnly. As Ian walked away, Elena glanced back at Alex, who was now scribbling furiously in a notepad. Her loyalty to the mission and her commitment to her friend were pulling her apart, but she knew that she couldn't turn her back on either one. The stakes were too high, and she had to find a way to balance both.

She took a deep breath, steeling herself for the challenges ahead. Whatever the outcome, she was determined to stand by her friend – even if it meant walking the tightrope between loyalty and duty.

The air in the control room was thick with tension, beads of sweat collecting on Alex's brow as they stood before Captain Singh and Dr. Hernandez. The usually pristine space was now a battleground for truth and paranoia, illuminated by the flickering lights of the control panel.

"Captain, it's time you knew the truth," Alex said, their voice trembling with conviction. "Dr. Hernandez has been manipulating our data, and both of you are part of a conspiracy to sabotage this mission."

Captain Singh sighed heavily, her eyes betraying the weight of the situation. She exchanged a concerned glance with Dr. Hernandez before addressing Alex. "Alex, we've been through this before. I assure you, there is no conspiracy. You need to trust us."

"Trust?" Alex spat out, their eyes wild with desperation. "How can I trust you when all the evidence points to the contrary?"

"Alex, please, listen to reason," Dr. Hernandez implored, his hands raised in a gesture of surrender. "You're misinterpreting innocent events as malicious intent. You're seeing ghosts where there are none."

But Alex was unyielding. Their fists clenched tightly at their sides, knuckles turning white. The more they were met with denial, the deeper they dug into their beliefs. Every fiber of their being screamed that something was amiss, and they couldn't let go of that nagging feeling.

"Enough!" Captain Singh barked, her patience wearing thin. "We have a mission to complete, and this... this obsession of yours is putting everyone at risk."

As the confrontation unfolded, the crew members watched with a mix of disbelief and apprehension. Whispers spread like wildfire, sparking doubt and discord among them. Some questioned the integrity of their leaders, while others feared for the success of their mission.

"Captain, I'm sorry, but I cannot stand idly by while this charade continues," Alex declared, their voice unwavering. "I will not let you and Dr. Hernandez jeopardize our mission."

Captain Singh's jaw clenched, her gaze fixed on Alex with a mixture of disappointment and concern. She recognized the gravity of the situation – Alex's accusations had fractured the crew's trust, and it was now her responsibility to mend those broken bonds.

"Very well, Alex," she said solemnly, her voice tinged with regret. "If you refuse to believe us, then we have no choice but to take action for the sake of the mission."

The control room fell silent as the weight of Captain Singh's words settled over them. The crew members exchanged uneasy glances, uncertainty gnawing at the edges of their minds. They knew that the mission's success hung in the balance, and they couldn't help but wonder what drastic measures might be taken to restore order.

As Alex stared defiantly into the eyes of Captain Singh and Dr. Hernandez, they felt the sting of betrayal. But even amid the chaos, there was one person who didn't waver in their support.

Elena stood by Alex's side, her loyalty unwavering despite the turmoil that churned within her. She knew that the path ahead would test the limits of their friendship, but as the control room erupted into a cacophony of dissent, she resolved to stand by Alex – no matter the cost.

The dim light of the control room seemed to flicker in time with the tension that hung heavy in the air. Elena, her back pressed against a cold metal panel, surveyed the crew as they whispered among themselves, their faces etched with uncertainty and fear. Some cast wary glances at Alex before turning away, as if the paranoia might be contagious. Elena clenched her fist, feeling the chill of isolation creeping in from all sides.

"Alex," she said softly, reaching out to touch their arm. "I believe you. Whatever it takes, we'll figure this out together."

Alex looked at her, desperation in their eyes. "Thank you, Elena. I don't know what I'd do without you."

Elena smiled reassuringly, but inside, her thoughts were in turmoil. She knew that supporting Alex meant standing against Captain Singh and Dr. Hernandez, two people she had always admired and trusted. Yet, her friendship with Alex held a deeper connection – one she couldn't simply abandon when things got tough.

"Hey, guys," interrupted Tara, one of the engineers, her voice laced with skepticism. "You really think there's some big conspiracy going on? Seems more like someone's just cracking under pressure."

Elena's jaw tightened as she held Tara's gaze. "We're not asking you to blindly follow us. But we have our reasons for believing something isn't right."

"Alex has reasons," corrected Michael, another crew member. "And now you're being dragged into this mess, too. Can't you see that?"

"Enough," snapped Elena, her patience wearing thin. "I've made my choice."

"Fine," Michael said, throwing his hands up in exasperation. "But when this all blows up, don't say we didn't warn you."

As he walked away, muttering to himself, Elena turned her attention back to Alex. Their eyes met, and for a moment, the chaos around them seemed to fade. They were in this together, bound by their shared suspicions and unwavering loyalty.

"Alex," Elena whispered, her voice full of determination. "We need a plan."

"Right," Alex agreed, nodding firmly. "We'll gather whatever evidence we can find and confront Singh and Hernandez again. They won't be able to deny the truth when it's staring them in the face."

Elena took a deep breath, steeling herself for the challenges ahead. She knew that supporting Alex would come at a cost – her relationships with the other crew members, her reputation, perhaps even her position on the mission. But she had made her choice, and she would not waver.

"Let's do it," she said, her resolve shining like a beacon in the darkness that threatened to consume them all. And with those words, she stepped further into the unknown, prepared to face whatever consequences lay ahead.
