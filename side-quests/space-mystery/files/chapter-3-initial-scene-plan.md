# Chapter 3 — initial scene plan

31 January 2024 · 14:44 · Supplied draft

1. Alex's mental state continues to deteriorate, with their paranoia and obsession with the Mars territorial dispute intensifying. Show their increasing agitation and erratic behaviour, such as pacing, muttering to themselves, and fixating on maps and news articles about the dispute.

2. Dr. Nadia Buhari notices the alarming changes in Alex's behaviour and becomes increasingly concerned for their well-being. Describe her observing Alex's actions from a distance, noting their growing isolation from the rest of the crew.

3. Dr. Nadia Buhari decides it is necessary to consult with Captain Priya Singh about the situation. Show her seeking out Captain Priya, finding her in her quarters reviewing mission reports.

4. Captain Priya listens attentively as Dr. Nadia Buhari expresses her concerns about Alex's mental health and the potential impact on the mission. Describe their conversation as a serious and urgent discussion, with Captain Priya asking probing questions about the specific behaviours and symptoms exhibited by Alex.

5. Dr. Nadia Buhari suggests that immediate intervention may be necessary to prevent further deterioration of Alex's mental state. Show her proposing the idea of confining Alex to their quarters for their safety and the safety of the crew.

6. Captain Priya acknowledges the gravity of the situation and the potential risks involved in confining a crew member. Describe her weighing the options and considering the potential consequences of taking such action.

7. Captain Priya and Dr. Nadia Buhari discuss the potential impact on crew morale and the delicate balance of maintaining a cohesive team. Show them debating the ethical implications of prioritizing the mission versus the well-being of an individual crew member.

8. Dr. Nadia Buhari emphasizes the importance of addressing Alex's mental health crisis promptly, highlighting the potential dangers of allowing the situation to escalate further. Show her presenting evidence and examples of past incidents where untreated mental health issues have led to disastrous outcomes in space missions.

9. Captain Priya ultimately agrees with Dr. Nadia Buhari's assessment and decides to implement the suggested intervention. Describe her instructing Dr. Nadia Buhari to confine Alex to their quarters and to monitor their condition closely.

10. Dr. Nadia Buhari expresses her gratitude to Captain Priya for taking the situation seriously and for prioritizing the well-being of the crew. Show her acknowledging the difficult decision Captain Priya had to make and her commitment to supporting the captain's leadership.

11. Captain Priya and Dr. Nadia Buhari discuss the importance of maintaining open lines of communication with the rest of the crew regarding Alex's situation. Show them strategizing on how to address any potential concerns or questions that may arise from the crew members.

12. Dr. Nadia Buhari leaves Captain Priya's quarters, determined to carry out the plan and provide the necessary support to Alex. Describe her walking purposefully towards Alex's quarters, mentally preparing herself for the challenging task ahead.

13. End the chapter with a sense of anticipation and tension, hinting at the potential consequences of confining Alex and the impact it may have on the crew dynamics and the mission as a whole.
