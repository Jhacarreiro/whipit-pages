# Review of the writer brief

30 January 2024 · 15:23 · AI review

The writer brief combines a Christie-style mystery with space exploration, psychological tension and international intrigue.

Revision options:

1. **Deepen Character Backstories**: Flesh out the characters more, especially the main and secondary characters. Provide more details about their past experiences, motivations, and how they ended up on this mission. This will add depth to their interactions and make their responses to the unfolding events more meaningful.

2. **Enhance World-Building Details**: While the setting is clearly futuristic and space-focused, adding more specific details about the world in this era can enrich the narrative. This could include descriptions of Earth's political and environmental state, advancements in everyday technology, and the societal impact of space colonization.

3. **Incorporate More Sci-Fi Elements**: While maintaining the mystery and psychological thriller tones, infuse more science fiction elements relevant to space travel and life in outer space. This could involve advanced AI, life-support systems, interstellar communication challenges, and descriptions of spacecraft and living conditions in space.

4. **Introduce Subtle Foreshadowing**: Early in the narrative, subtly foreshadow key plot twists and revelations. This can be done through seemingly innocuous details or dialogues that gain significance as the story progresses.

5. **Build Tension with Tighter Pacing**: Adjust the pacing to build tension effectively. In some parts of the story, especially as the mysteries start to unravel, quicker pacing can heighten suspense and keep readers engaged.

6. **Clarify the Technological and Ethical Dilemmas**: Expand on the technological and ethical dilemmas presented in the story. This could involve debates among the crew, insights from Earth-based characters, or media excerpts showing the public's perception of the mission and the technology involved.

7. **Expand on Resolution and Aftermath**: Provide a more detailed resolution and aftermath, showing not just the immediate consequences of the revelations but also their longer-term impact on the characters, the space program, and international relations.

8. **Introduce an Element of Romance or Personal Relationships**: To add another layer of complexity, introduce subplots involving personal relationships or romance among the crew members or between the crew and characters on Earth. This can provide a humanizing element to the story and offer a break from the high-stakes tension.
