# Chapter one — the morning, the café and the embassy

A faint glow of the morning sun pierced the heavy curtains in Thomas Harrington's apartment, casting a sliver of light across the room. The 45-year-old British diplomat stirred in his bed, still tormented by dreams that clawed at the remnants of his past life. Memories of his two decades in intelligence tightened their grip, threatening to consume him even in waking moments. But today was another day in Kyiv, Ukraine, beckoning with fresh challenges and weighty responsibilities.

As he peeled back the covers and swung his legs over the side of the bed, his thoughts fluidly switched between languages. "Time to rise," he muttered, his words carrying the weight of determination.

Stationed in Kyiv for several months now, Thomas found solace in the structure of his daily routine amidst the city's chaotic existence. Each day began much like the previous one: a steaming mug of tea, a quick scan of the local and international headlines, and a brisk walk to the British embassy. Stability embraced him in the predictable cadence of these rituals.

"Good morning, Mr. Harrington," greeted Oksana, the ever-diligent receptionist at the embassy, her smile radiating warmth and familiarity. "Your schedule is quite full today. You have meetings with the Ukrainian deputy foreign minister and several local officials."

"Thank you, Oksana," Thomas replied, allowing a thin smile to curve his lips. "Busy days keep us on our toes, don't they?"

"Indeed, they do," she agreed, handing him a meticulously typed itinerary.

As Thomas maneuvered through the labyrinthine halls of the embassy, he contemplated the evolution of his role since arriving in Ukraine. On the surface, he wore the mask of a diplomat, navigating social functions and building connections with officials. Yet beneath that polished veneer lay a clandestine purpose: gathering intelligence for MI6.

His days were a balancing act of diplomacy and covert pursuits. While engaging in handshakes and pleasantries, his ears remained attuned to the whispers of information that could serve his home country. It was a delicate dance, requiring finesse and unwavering dedication to the mission.

"Mr. Harrington," began the Ukrainian deputy foreign minister, extending his hand with practiced warmth as they settled down to discuss matters of state. "We are deeply grateful for your country's support during these challenging times."

"You have our unwavering commitment to ensuring a stable and prosperous Ukraine," Thomas replied, his gaze fixed on the other man, searching for any hint of underlying intentions. He wondered what lay concealed in the shadows of diplomacy, what secrets lurked behind the polite façade.

Throughout the day, Thomas wove his way through the intricate tapestry of international politics, the affable diplomat's mask firmly in place. Yet beneath that polished exterior, he collected intelligence with the precision of a seasoned operative. As the sun descended, casting its golden glow over Kyiv, he returned to his apartment, mentally cataloguing the valuable information gleaned during the day.

The sun had barely risen when Thomas found himself seated in a discreet corner of a bustling Kyiv café. Clutching a steaming cup of coffee, his fingers lightly brushed its warmth as he observed the room with the discerning eye of a seasoned professional.

"Доброе утро, мистер Харрингтон," greeted the waiter, a young man with an infectious smile and eyes that revealed a keen intellect.

"Доброе утро," Thomas effortlessly replied in Russian, his accent flawless. The waiter nodded approvingly before withdrawing, leaving Thomas to continue his vigilant observation of the patrons.

A familiar face caught his attention – Oksana, a high-ranking official in the Ukrainian Ministry of Internal Affairs. Approaching him, her heels clicking on the marble floor, she switched to French, a language that offered a measure of privacy in their conversation.

"Bonjour, Monsieur Harrington," she said, extending her hand.

"Bonjour, Madame Zaytseva," Thomas replied, taking her hand and offering a polite smile. Their eyes met briefly, a silent understanding passing between them. These clandestine meetings had become an integral part of his work in Kyiv.

"J'ai des informations pour vous," Oksana whispered, her voice barely audible above the hum of surrounding conversations. Adrenaline surged through Thomas, the prospect of acquiring new intelligence always quickening his pulse.

"Alors, parlez-moi," he urged, his tone measured and calm, masking the racing pulse beneath his composed exterior.

As Oksana divulged the latest political developments, Thomas couldn't help but admire her courage. He recognized the risks she undertook by sharing this information with him, and it reinforced the significance of his work – not solely for Britain but for the people of Ukraine.

"Merci, Madame Zaytseva," Thomas murmured, genuine gratitude in his voice. "Votre aide est inestimable."

"Prenez soin de vous, Monsieur Harrington," Oksana replied, her voice tinged with concern. With a curt nod, she disappeared into the crowd.

Sipping his coffee, Thomas contemplated the invaluable information he had acquired. He knew it would be of great value to his team back home, and a surge of pride swelled within him for the contribution he could make. Moments like these reaffirmed his purpose – utilizing his linguistic prowess and astute comprehension of the political landscape to navigate high-stakes situations and unearth vital intelligence.

However, he needed to return to the embassy now and share his findings. As he rose from his seat and left the café, a weight settled on his shoulders – the weight of responsibility. Yet with each step, he renewed his commitment to the mission and the people who depended on him.

Walking back to the embassy, a gust of wind rustled past, carrying the scent of damp earth and wet leaves. Its unmistakable aroma triggered a flood of memories from a distant time – a memory Thomas had struggled to bury. The streets of Kyiv blurred before him, his mind slipping back to a botched operation in Chechnya.

"Trust me, Thomas," a voice echoed in his head, the voice of a former colleague he had once considered a friend. The sting of betrayal still lingered, even after all these years.

Shaking his head to clear the intrusive thoughts, Thomas focused on the steady rhythm of his footsteps against the pavement. He couldn't allow his past to interfere with his present duties in Ukraine; too much was at stake.

"Evening, Mr. Harrington," the doorman greeted him as he entered the embassy. "Anything interesting happen today?"

"Nothing out of the ordinary, Ivan," Thomas replied, forcing a smile. His tone appeared light, yet weariness lingered in his eyes, transcending mere physical fatigue.

"Good, good," Ivan said, oblivious to Thomas's inner turmoil. "I'll be here if you need anything."

"Thank you, Ivan," Thomas murmured, his thoughts already drawn back to the ghosts that haunted him.

Moving through the embassy's dimly lit corridors, Thomas couldn't help but recall how his past experiences seeped into his present existence. His mind wandered to his estranged wife and daughter in London, the countless nights drowned in whiskey as he tried desperately to escape the burden of guilt and regret.

"Thomas!" a voice called out, breaking through his reverie. He looked up to see his superior, Claire, approaching with a concerned expression. "You look like you've seen a ghost. Are you alright?"

"Of course, Claire," he replied, offering her a tight-lipped smile. "Just a bit tired, that's all."

"Alright," she said hesitantly, studying his face a moment longer before continuing. "Well, I have some updates on our current situation that we need to discuss. Can you join me in my office?"

"Absolutely," Thomas said, following her down the hall. As they walked, he focused on the task at hand, suppressing the uneasy feeling that had settled in his gut. He couldn't allow his past to define him, not when there was so much work to be done and so many lives depending on him.

Yet deep down, he feared that no matter how hard he tried to forge ahead, the specters of his past would never truly release their hold. Entering Claire's office, ready to employ his skills once again, Thomas couldn't help but wonder if he would ever find a way to escape the enduring shadows that haunted him.
