# Chapter 2 — supplied draft

31 January 2024 · 14:33 · Supplied draft

The dim lights cast a melancholic glow on the spacecraft's narrow corridors, reflecting Alex Mendoza's furrowed brow as they cautiously navigated their way through. Every step they took seemed to echo back at them, further fueling the paranoia that had taken root in their mind. It was as if the ship itself had become an adversary, its soft whirring and rhythmic hums amplifying rather than soothing their growing anxiety.

"Keep it together, Alex," they muttered under their breath, trying to focus on the task at hand.

As they continued with their routine checks, the lights above suddenly flickered unnaturally, casting long shadows that danced along the walls like sinister apparitions. Alex froze, their heart pounding in their chest, as they tried to make sense of what had just happened.

"Must be a power surge," they reasoned, attempting to quell their fears with logic. But deep down, they couldn't shake the feeling that something was amiss. It was as though the ship was sending them a message, daring them to unravel its secrets.

"Hey, Alex," a voice called out, snapping them back to reality. It was one of their fellow crew members, concern etched on their face. "You alright? You look a little spooked."

"I'm fine," Alex replied hastily, forcing a smile. "Just thought I saw something, but it was nothing. Really."

"Alright, but if you need someone to talk to, I'm here," the crew member offered before continuing down the corridor.

"Thanks," Alex whispered, grateful for the offer but unwilling to burden anyone else with their mounting paranoia.

As they resumed their checks, Alex couldn't help but replay the bizarre incident in their head. They knew they had to find answers or risk succumbing to the darkness that threatened to consume them. And so, with renewed determination, they pressed on, each step taking them further into the heart of the enigma that was now their life.

The soft glow of the dimly lit workstation reflected in Alex's eyes as they bent over a thick notebook, pen in hand. Each entry was marked with military precision: date, time, location, and duration of every anomaly they had experienced. The constant hum of the engine provided an eerie soundtrack to their obsession.

"Alex?" Elena's voice cut through the silence like a lifeline, her Russian accent instantly recognizable.

"Hey," Alex replied, barely looking up from their work. Their fingers tapped on the edge of the table in an anxious rhythm.

"Working late again?" Elena asked, concern evident in her tone.

"Always," Alex responded, forcing a smile. "I've been documenting these…anomalies."

"Anomalies?" Elena raised an eyebrow, intrigued. She moved closer, her dark hair framing her face as she peered at the notebook. "What kind of anomalies?"

"Strange things happening around the ship," Alex admitted, finally meeting Elena's gaze. They hesitated before confiding further, "You know, like that flickering light I mentioned the other day. And there have been others too—unexplained sounds, temperature fluctuations, even objects moving seemingly on their own."

Elena's expression shifted, a shadow of recognition passing over her face. "I have seen some strange things myself," she admitted, her voice low.

"Really?" Alex leaned in closer, the weight of loneliness lifting ever so slightly. "I thought it was just me."

"Of course not," Elena reassured them, placing a comforting hand on Alex's shoulder. "We are all under immense pressure out here in the vastness of space. It is natural for our minds to play tricks on us. But if there is something more to it, we should be vigilant."

"Exactly," Alex nodded vigorously, grateful for the validation. "That's why I'm doing this." They gestured at the notebook, filled with page after page of meticulously recorded details. "If I can find a pattern or a cause, maybe we can figure out what's happening and put an end to it."

"Alex, I want you to know that I am here for you," Elena said, her eyes full of sincerity. "I understand your need for answers and will do everything in my power to help you find them."

"Thank you, Elena," Alex whispered, their voice thick with emotion. "It means more to me than you'll ever know."

As they shared a moment of silent understanding, the ship seemed to close in around them, the darkness pressing against the walls like a living thing. They knew they were not alone in their quest for truth, but the fear still lingered, gnawing at the edges of their minds. Together, they vowed to confront the unexplained, whatever it may be, and unravel the mysteries hidden within the heart of the spacecraft.

Elena's face, bathed in the pale glow of the console, seemed to flicker like a dying candle as she listened to Alex's account. Her eyes widened with concern, but there was also an unmistakable glimmer of recognition.

"Alex, you're not alone," she said softly, leaning closer. "I've had some... unsettling experiences as well. Strange noises, fleeting shadows – things I couldn't quite explain."

"Really?" Alex's pulse quickened at this subtle validation. "Do you think it could be related to the Mars dispute? Maybe someone's trying to sabotage our mission?"

"Perhaps," Elena replied, her voice hesitant. She glanced over her shoulder, as if fearing eavesdroppers. "But let's not jump to conclusions. We need more evidence before we can make any accusations."

"Right, of course." Alex's fingers drummed nervously on the table. They needed to stay rational. To find patterns amidst the chaos. "Just promise me you'll let me know if anything else happens, okay?"

"Of course, Alex. You have my word."

Suddenly, the spacecraft shuddered, and the lights flickered more violently than ever before. Alarms blared, and the hum of the engine grew louder, more insistent. The crew members stumbled from their quarters, eyes wide with panic.

"Alex! Elena!" The captain's voice crackled over the intercom. "Report to the control room immediately. We have a situation."

"Damn it," Alex muttered under their breath. Heart pounding, they exchanged a worried glance with Elena. This new disturbance was too vivid, too chilling to be mere coincidence. It felt like a warning – a harbinger of something far more sinister lurking in the shadows.

"Let's go," Elena urged, her eyes hard with determination. As they rushed through the dim corridors, the ship's operational systems seemed to falter and rebel, each unexplained malfunction heightening the crew's unease.

"Stay focused," Alex thought, forcing their breaths to slow. "We need answers. We need to know what's happening, and why."

But as they reached the control room, the weight of uncertainty pressed down on them like a suffocating blanket. They felt the darkness closing in, threatening to swallow them whole. And for the first time in their life, Alex Mendoza feared that the truth might be more dangerous than the unknown.

The dim hum of the control room's monitors cast a cold, blue glow on Alex's face as they scanned the data for any discernible pattern. Their eyes darted between the screens, fingers tapping impatiently on the console. The blurred lines of code and diagnostics threatened to merge into unintelligible chaos, but Alex persisted.

"Alex, you need to rest," Elena said, her voice tinged with concern as she leaned against the doorway. "You've been at this non-stop since we left the Elysium Sector."

"Rest?" Alex scoffed, their interior thoughts simmering with determination. "How can I rest when something is happening to this ship? To us? There must be an answer, Elena. We just need to find it."

"Maybe there isn't an answer. Not everything has an explanation." Elena sighed, crossing her arms and watching her friend warily. "Have you considered that maybe your mind is playing tricks on you?"

A flicker of doubt clouded Alex's expression. They paused, considering the possibility that their paranoia was merely a symptom of the stress from the mission. Space could be isolating, unforgiving even to the most resilient minds. But Alex shook off the thought, unwilling to concede to doubts.

"No, Elena. This isn't just my imagination running wild. The malfunctions, the anomalies...they're real. And they started happening after we left the Elysium Sector. There must be a connection."

"Alright," Elena relented, her gaze softening with empathy. "What do you need me to do?"

"Help me go through the logs again," Alex replied, their conviction rekindling. "I'll look for patterns in the malfunctions while you check for anything unusual in the crew's activities. We may find a correlation."

Elena nodded and pulled up a chair beside Alex. Together, they delved into the labyrinth of data, hunting for any thread that could unravel the mystery surrounding them.

"Alex," Elena said after a while, her voice hesitant. "Do you ever wonder if we're in over our heads? If what we find might be…dangerous?"

"Every day," Alex admitted, allowing themselves a moment of vulnerability. "But we can't let fear hold us back from discovering the truth. Whatever's happening on this ship, it affects all of us. We owe it to the crew to figure this out."

In the chilling silence of the control room, their determination burned like a beacon, guiding their search through the vast expanse of uncertainty. As they sifted through the layers of information, Alex fought against the creeping self-doubt and the isolation of space, driven by an unwavering resolve to uncover the truth – no matter how treacherous it may be.

The dim glow of the computer screen illuminated Alex's face, casting eerie shadows that danced across their features as they delved deeper into the ship's logs. Their fingers flew over the keyboard, searching for any hint of a clue, a pattern amidst the jumble of data. The soft hum of the ship's life support systems seemed to pulsate in time with Alex's racing heartbeat, fueling an undercurrent of paranoia that swallowed them whole.

"Got it," Elena whispered triumphantly, breaking the silence. "I found something odd in the maintenance records."

"Let me see." Alex leaned closer, scrutinizing the lines of text on the screen. There, nestled between routine entries, was a coded message—one that had no business being there. The hairs on the back of their neck stood on end as they considered the implications.

"Has anyone from the crew ever reported seeing this?" Alex asked, their voice tight with tension.

Elena shook her head. "Not to my knowledge. It's like it was hidden here, waiting for someone to find it."

"Or not find it," Alex muttered, their mind racing. What did this mean? Was it a warning, or a breadcrumb left by someone who knew more than they should?

"Can you decipher it?" Elena questioned, her eyes locked onto the screen.

"I can try." With feverish determination, Alex worked on cracking the code, their fingers tapping out a staccato rhythm that echoed through the otherwise silent room. As the characters transformed into coherent words and phrases, Alex's heart sank.

"It says... 'Project Elysium: Security Compromised. Proceed with caution,'" Alex read aloud, their voice barely audible. A chill ran down their spine as they realized the gravity of the situation.

"Security compromised?" Elena repeated, her brow furrowing with concern. "What does that mean for us?"

"Nothing good," Alex admitted grimly. "We need to dig deeper, find out what's going on before—"

"Alex! Elena!" The urgent voice of Captain Reynolds crackled through the intercom, interrupting their investigation. "Report to the main deck immediately. We have a situation."

"Damn it," Alex cursed under their breath, hastily minimizing the logs and coded message. Glancing at Elena, they saw the same mix of frustration and fear reflected in her eyes. "We'll have to pick this up later."

"Right," Elena agreed, her voice strained. "Let's see what the captain needs."

Together, they hurried from the control room, leaving behind the faint glow of the computer screen and the secrets it held. As they navigated the ship's dim corridors, Alex's thoughts swirled like a vortex, each new revelation threatening to consume them. And yet, amidst the chaos, one thing was certain: whatever was happening aboard the spacecraft, it was anything but routine.

Alex's heart raced as they stood on the main deck, their eyes darting around the room, searching for any sign of what had prompted the captain's urgent call. Their mind churned with the implications of Project Elysium and the compromised security, but now was not the time to dwell on it.

"Alright, everyone," Captain Reynolds addressed the crew, his voice firm yet laced with tension. "We've got a malfunction in the ship's gravitational stabilizers. I need you all to split into teams and check every system component connected to them."

Their task assigned, Alex sought out Elena amidst the bustling crew members. "Meet me in the engineering access corridor after we're done here," they whispered, barely audible over the cacophony of voices. "We can't let this derail our investigation."

"Got it," Elena replied, her expression steely with resolve.

***

Hours later, sweat beaded on Alex's forehead as they emerged from the cramped maintenance shafts. The gravitational issue resolved, their thoughts immediately returned to the coded message and the unsettling truth it concealed.

"Alex," Elena approached, her voice hushed as she handed them a small, portable data drive. "I managed to download the restricted mission files while we were working. I thought it might help our investigation."

"Excellent thinking," Alex praised, sliding the drive into their pocket. "Let's find a secure place to access this."

They moved stealthily through the dimly lit corridors, eventually settling on an abandoned storage room. As the door hissed shut behind them, Alex connected the drive to a handheld tablet, its screen casting a faint glow across their faces.

"Let's see what secrets are hidden here," Alex muttered, scrolling through the files with growing anticipation. Elena leaned in, her breath warm against Alex's cheek as they delved deeper into the classified information.

"Wait, look at this," Elena pointed at a document labeled 'Contingency Plan E-7'. "It mentions Project Elysium and... Oh, no."

"Is that what I think it is?" Alex's voice trembled as they read the chilling details of a possible experiment involving the crew.

"Seems like it," Elena confirmed gravely. "We need to find out more about this project before we can confront the captain."

"Agreed," Alex said. "We'll have to access the ship's central database, maybe even hack into the captain's personal files. It's risky, but it's our only chance to get answers."

"Let's do it," Elena nodded, determination etched on her face. "Together."

As they formulated their plan, neither of them noticed the shadowy figure lurking just outside the storage room, their presence masked by the soft hum of the ship's systems. A whispered conversation between the mysterious observer and an unseen accomplice floated through the air, carried by the ever-present artificial breeze.

"Keep a close eye on them," the hidden voice instructed. "If they find out too much, we'll have to initiate Contingency Plan E-7 immediately."

The shadowy figure's eyes narrowed, watching Alex and Elena with cold calculation. Their silent agreement sent a shiver down the spine of the ship itself, foreshadowing the dire consequences that awaited the unsuspecting crew.
