# Telling Marie about the event — second try

"Ah, the event," Thomas began, his gaze unconsciously flickering to the horizon. He cleared his throat, bringing his attention back to Marie. "It was... enlightening."

Marie tilted her head, her eyes sparkling with interest. "Enlightening? Now that's a word I don't hear often from you, Thomas."

Thomas chuckled, setting his teacup on the balcony railing. He briefly considered telling her about the woman in the black dress, but dismissed the thought as quickly as it came. He knew better than to voice his curiosities prematurely.

"There was a considerable gathering of key figures," Thomas offered, turning his gaze back to Marie. "Plenty of discussions on trade agreements, cultural exchanges and the like, but what truly piqued my interest were the undertones... the unspoken messages."

A knowing smile graced Marie's face. "Ah, the language of diplomacy. It's not about what's said, but what's left unsaid, right?"

"Right you are," Thomas affirmed, his mind involuntarily circling back to the woman in the black dress. Who was she? What was her role in this intricate web of politics and power? He couldn't deny the unsettling feeling she'd instilled in him. It was as if she'd silently beckoned him into a mystery yet to be unfolded. But he couldn't let on that to Marie, not yet.

"Sounds like you've got a mystery to solve, Thomas," Marie teased, lightly bumping his shoulder. "Anyone I should be worried about?" she asked in jest.

Thomas smiled at her playful tone, raising an eyebrow in mock surprise. "Worried? Marie, I thought you knew me better than that. I love a good mystery."

"Indeed you do," Marie said, her smile echoing his own. She moved away from the balcony door, heading back into the office. "Don't keep me in suspense for too long, Thomas."

Thomas watched her leave, his mind once again returning to the woman in the black dress. He was intrigued, that much was certain. There was a story there, a piece of the puzzle that was Kyiv's ever-shifting political landscape.

But that was a thought for another day. For now, he had enough on his plate, deciphering the coded language of diplomatic conversations and understanding the unspoken messages. He refilled his cup, staring out into the vibrant city as the day gave way to night. The game was on, and Thomas Harrington was more than ready to play.
