# Chapter one — a rewrite in three parts

1 - 2

As the morning sun peeked through the heavy curtains, Thomas Harrington stirred in his bed, tormented by memories that clawed at him from his past life. The weight of his two decades in intelligence gnawed at his soul, threatening to consume him. But in Kyiv, Ukraine, another day of challenges awaited him.

With a muttered "Time to rise" in multiple languages, Thomas threw back the covers and swung his legs over the edge of the bed. The routine of his daily life in Kyiv brought a sense of stability amidst the city's chaos. A cup of steaming tea, a glance at the newspapers, and a walk to the British embassy set the rhythm for the day.

"Good morning, Mr. Harrington," greeted Oksana, the diligent receptionist at the embassy. Her warm smile welcomed him as always. "Your schedule is packed today. Meetings with the Ukrainian deputy foreign minister and local officials."

"Thank you, Oksana," Thomas replied, offering a thin smile. "Busy days keep us on our toes, don't they?"

"Yes, indeed," she agreed, handing him an itinerary.

As Thomas navigated the embassy's halls, he pondered the evolution of his role in Ukraine. Beyond the façade of a diplomat, he discreetly gathered intelligence for MI6. Amidst handshakes and pleasantries, he listened for whispers that could prove valuable. It was a delicate dance, requiring finesse and unwavering commitment.

"Mr. Harrington," began the Ukrainian deputy foreign minister, extending his hand with practiced warmth. They sat down to discuss matters of state, and Thomas searched for hidden meanings behind their words. He wondered what lay beneath the surface, what secrets diplomacy concealed.

Throughout the day, Thomas maintained his diplomat persona, collecting intelligence while rubbing elbows with politicians. As the sun painted Kyiv in gold hues, he returned to his apartment, mentally processing the information he had gathered.

3 - 4

In a bustling Kyiv café, Thomas sat in a discreet corner, coffee in hand, observing the patrons with a seasoned eye. The waiter, acknowledging Thomas's linguistic prowess, greeted him with "Good morning, Mr. Harrington."

"Good morning," Thomas effortlessly replied in Russian. The waiter nodded approvingly and left him to his observations.

Oksana, a high-ranking official, caught Thomas's eye. Switching to French for privacy, they exchanged greetings.

"I have information for you," Oksana whispered, her voice barely audible amidst the café's hum. Thomas's adrenaline spiked, ready for new intelligence.

"Tell me," he urged, maintaining a calm demeanor despite his racing pulse.

As Oksana shared the latest political developments, Thomas admired her courage and the significance of his work for both Britain and Ukraine.

"Thank you, Madame Zaytseva," Thomas murmured sincerely. "Your assistance is invaluable."

"Take care, Mr. Harrington," Oksana replied with a nod, disappearing into the crowd.

Savoring his coffee, Thomas mulled over the information. It would be vital to his team back home, reinforcing his sense of purpose. His linguistic skills and political understanding drove him to navigate high-stakes situations and uncover critical intelligence.

Now, he needed to return to the embassy and share his findings. With each step, the weight of responsibility settled on his shoulders. But his commitment to the mission and the people who relied on him remained resolute.
