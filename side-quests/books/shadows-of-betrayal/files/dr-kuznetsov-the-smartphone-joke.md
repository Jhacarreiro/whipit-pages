# Dr. Kuznetsov — the smartphone joke

In an office where the aroma of old books and academic air collided, Thomas Harrington found himself sitting across from the esteemed psychiatrist, Dr. Ivan Kuznetsov. Dr. Kuznetsov’s office was a homage to nostalgia, with its walls lined with dusty books and the scent of countless past clients soaked into the antique leather chair in which Thomas sat.

Dr. Kuznetsov, a man who spoke more with his expressive eyebrows than with his lips, was scribbling away at his notepad. His eyes flickered up to Thomas over his half-moon glasses, a barely noticeable smirk playing at his lips.

"Thomas," Ivan began, his voice carrying a hint of amusement, "I must admit, your thoughts sometimes feel like navigating a flea market on a Sunday. There's always something unexpected to discover."

Thomas raised an eyebrow and chuckled. "I'll take that as a compliment, Ivan. But speaking of discoveries, I might have one that could rival the mystery of the Bermuda Triangle."

Ivan let out a hearty laugh, putting down his notepad. "Oh, this I have to hear," he declared, leaning back in his chair.

"Well," Thomas began, looking around the room thoughtfully, "I've started noticing people staring at their hands a lot. Almost as if they've never seen their fingers before!"

Ivan stared at him for a moment before bursting into laughter. "Thomas, that's called a 'smartphone'. Welcome to the 21st century!"

Thomas joined in the laughter, his tension easing off a bit. "Well, who knew? Maybe I should get one of these 'smart' phones to fit in. Though I bet it wouldn't know a good cup of tea if it fell into one!"

Their laughter echoed through the room, the heavy tension replaced with camaraderie. The complex psyche of Thomas Harrington, the web of political intrigue he navigated daily, was momentarily pushed to the side as the room filled with infectious laughter. If only every psychiatric consultation could be this amusing!
