What Dmitri could reveal

AI draft. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Download text ↓

Dmitri Orlov could provide Natalia and Thomas with a crucial piece of information that serves as a breadcrumb leading them closer to the truth. This information could include:

  1. A secret meeting: Dmitri hints at a clandestine gathering involving key players in the conspiracy. He provides a vague location and time, leaving Natalia and Thomas to decipher the details and infiltrate the meeting to gather more evidence.

  2. A coded message: Dmitri cryptically mentions a coded message that holds important information about the conspiracy. He provides them with a partial deciphering key or a clue to help them crack the code, setting them on a path of decoding and uncovering hidden truths.

  3. A contact: Dmitri reveals the existence of a high-ranking insider who has knowledge of the conspiracy's inner workings. He gives them a name or a codename, urging them to find this contact and extract the information they need to expose the conspiracy.

  4. A hidden document: Dmitri hints at the existence of a hidden document that contains incriminating evidence. He provides a description of the document's hiding place or a clue that leads Natalia and Thomas to a specific location where they can uncover this crucial piece of evidence.

By providing Natalia and Thomas with such information, Dmitri sets them on a new investigative path, propelling the story forward as they follow leads, unravel secrets, and confront new challenges in their quest for the truth.
