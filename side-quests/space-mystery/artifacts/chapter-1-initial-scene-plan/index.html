Chapter 1 — initial scene plan

31 January 2024 · 09:38·Draft for review

Download this document ↓
  1. Introduce Captain Priya Singh as she oversees the final preparations for the Mars mission, displaying her calm demeanor and strong leadership skills.
  2. Show Dr. Leo Hernandez troubleshooting technical issues on the spacecraft, highlighting his analytical mind and pragmatic approach.
  3. Depict Alex Mendoza's intense focus and determination as they prepare for the mission, hinting at their troubled past and desire for redemption.
  4. Highlight Elena Kozlova's role in maintaining communication with Earth, emphasizing her sympathy towards Alex's plight and her hidden secret related to the mission.
  5. Introduce Dr. Yuto Takahashi conducting research on the spacecraft, showcasing his keen observational skills and scientific curiosity.
  6. Show Dr. Nadia Buhari ensuring the crew's physical and mental well-being, emphasizing her empathy and understanding of trauma.
  7. Portray Zara Al-Farsi as she interacts with the crew, representing the interests of the international consortium funding the mission.
  8. Establish the diverse backgrounds and nationalities of the crew members, highlighting the global cooperation behind the mission.
  9. Depict the crew members interacting with each other, showcasing their initial camaraderie and excitement for the mission.
  10. Show glimpses of Alex's deteriorating mental state, hinting at their growing paranoia and obsessions.
  11. Highlight the crew's reactions to Alex's behavior, with some expressing concern while others remain skeptical.
  12. Establish the sense of anticipation and excitement as the crew prepares to embark on the Mars mission, setting the stage for the challenges and conflicts to come.
