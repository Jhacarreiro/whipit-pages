The bicycle and the fairies

English translation of the Portuguese source. A separate working scene from the Rexi adaptation. The archive preserves changes in voice, props and continuity between versions.

Download text ↓

With sparkling eyes and a smile on my face, I could hardly wait to try my new bicycle, the perfect present to go with my magical slippers that left glowing footprints. In the garden, beneath a starry sky, I began my adventure. The slippers lit the way as I pedalled happily, creating a show of light. The feeling of freedom was wonderful; every lap filled me with joy.

Halfway around, the fairies I had met earlier appeared, dancing around me, fascinated by the glowing footprints. With a touch of magic, they made the light from my slippers shine even brighter, making the night more special still. Together, we celebrated the beauty of the moment, bringing the fairies' enchantment together with the joy of my new bicycle.

That magical night, with the bicycle of my dreams, the glowing slippers and the company of the fairies, promised unforgettable adventures. I knew that wherever I went, I would leave a trail of magic behind me.
