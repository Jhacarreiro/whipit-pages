A spaceship in the living room

English translation of the Portuguese source. A separate working scene from the Rexi adaptation. The archive preserves changes in voice, props and continuity between versions.

Download text ↓

His eyes sparkling with excitement after following the trail of shimmering stones, Rexi finds himself in front of an empty space in the living room, perfect for his next great adventure. Inspired by the science-fiction films he loves so much, he decides it is time to build his own spaceship.

He begins gathering everything he needs: cardboard boxes big enough to form the ship's base, toilet-roll tubes for the engines, saucepan lids for the controls, and aluminium foil to give it that futuristic touch. With an astronaut's determination, Rexi throws himself into the construction, folding, cutting and gluing, while his magical slippers light the way, making every detail of the build even more special.

As the ship takes shape, Rexi imagines the distant galaxies he will explore, the unknown planets he will discover, and the aliens he might befriend. The living room becomes a space hangar, and at its centre stands Rexi's spaceship, ready to take off.

Proud of his creation, Rexi climbs aboard, puts on a helmet made from an old shoebox, and gets ready for the countdown. "3, 2, 1... Lift-off!" Rexi's imagination is powerful, and as the ship "takes flight", the adventures waiting for him in the vast universe are as endless as the stars in the sky.
