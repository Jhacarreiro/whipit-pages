Synopsis — first review

31 January 2024 · 09:23·AI review

Download this document ↓

The synopsis combines psychological thriller, science fiction and geopolitical conflict. The revision priorities cover character stakes, scientific detail and the consequences of the resolution.

  1. Character Depth: The brief introductions to the crew members are engaging and promise a richly diverse cast, each with their unique perspective and role in the unfolding drama. Consider hinting at deeper backstories or personal stakes to enhance reader investment in their journeys.

  2. Psychological Thriller Elements: The focus on Alex's psychological unraveling is intriguing and sets a tense atmosphere. Amplifying the mystery and suspense around Alex's condition and its impact on the mission could heighten the thriller aspect.

  3. Geopolitical and Corporate Dynamics: The mention of geopolitical motives, corporate interests, and the rivalry between India and China adds a complex layer to the narrative. Further exploration of how these elements influence the mission and the crew's interactions could provide a richer context and elevate the stakes.

  4. Mars Territorial Conflict: This subplot is a smart inclusion, mirroring real-world geopolitical tensions in a space exploration setting. Expanding on how this conflict influences the crew's mission and personal dynamics could offer additional layers of conflict and intrigue.

  5. Scientific and Technological Detail: Given the setting, incorporating more specific scientific and technological details could enhance the realism and immersion of the story. This could involve descriptions of the spacecraft, Mars habitat, or the technological advancements that have made such missions feasible.

  6. Resolution and Themes: The proposed resolution involving a critical event that forces cooperation is a strong narrative choice, potentially leading to a climactic convergence of the novel's themes. Delving into the aftermath of such an event could provide a satisfying conclusion and reflect on the broader themes of cooperation, survival, and human ambition.
