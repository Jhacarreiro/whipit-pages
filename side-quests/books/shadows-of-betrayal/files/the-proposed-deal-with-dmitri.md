# The proposed deal with Dmitri

Over the following weeks, Thomas and Natalia worked tirelessly to rebuild their investigation. They sifted through mountains of paperwork, retraced suspicious transactions, and connected dots that led back to an intricate web of corruption that had far-reaching implications. Their progress was hard-won but ultimately worth it. Piece by piece, they began to understand the extent of Dmitri Orlov's machinations and the depth of his influence.

Finally, after countless sleepless nights and seemingly endless dead ends, they had compiled enough evidence to expose the corruption scandal that reached the highest echelons of Ukrainian politics.

"We've done it," Natalia said, gazing at the completed investigative report sprawled across the table. "This is bigger than we initially thought, Thomas."

"Yes, it is," he replied, a sense of grim satisfaction etched on his face. "We can't back down now. Orlov's corruption has hurt too many people. It's time we put an end to this."

Natalia nodded, determination burning bright in her eyes. She began to draft the article that would expose the corruption scheme and the key players involved, including Dmitri Orlov.

Just as Natalia was preparing to submit her article for publication, Thomas's encrypted phone vibrated with an incoming message. He read the contents and paled. "It's from Orlov," he said, holding out the phone for Natalia to read.

The message was chillingly simple: "A deal: my name stays out of the press, and you get to keep breathing."

A chill ran down Natalia's spine, but she straightened her back, resolute. "He's scared," she said. "We've cornered him."

"We knew this was coming," Thomas added, his eyes hardening. "We knew that once we got close enough, he'd try something. But we won't be intimidated."

"What do we do now?" Natalia asked.

"We carry on," Thomas replied. "We have a responsibility to those affected by Orlov's actions. We don't negotiate with criminals."

Natalia nodded in agreement. The stakes were high, but they were more committed than ever. "We've come too far to back down now," she declared.

"Exactly," Thomas affirmed. "Let's finish what we started."

Together, they pressed forward, undeterred by the threats and intimidation tactics. They were prepared to face whatever came their way to bring justice to those wronged by Dmitri Orlov's corrupt practices.
