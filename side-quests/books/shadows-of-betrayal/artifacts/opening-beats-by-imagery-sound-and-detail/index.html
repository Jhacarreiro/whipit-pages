Scene plan · 2023-09-06

Opening beats by imagery, sound and detail

AI draft. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Download text ↓

  1. Introducing Thomas Harrington (130 words): - Imagery: Describe the dull hum of the plane's engines slowing down. Zoom into the foot of a well-traveled man stepping onto Ukrainian soil. - Detail: Narrate the subtle reactions of those around him. Perhaps a flight attendant momentarily loses her professional demeanor, caught by his tall and authoritative stance.

  2. Kyiv's Atmosphere (140 words): - Imagery: The city alive with contrasts: age-old cathedrals amidst modern boutiques; children chasing pigeons while businessmen rush past. - Sound: The distant melody of a Ukrainian folk song juxtaposed with contemporary beats from a nearby café.

  3. Thomas's Initial Impressions (130 words): - Internal Dialogue: His seasoned instincts making notes — the vibrancy disguising the underlying tension, much like many places he’s been before. - Sensory Details: The aroma of food stalls, the slightly cooler air signaling the onset of evening.

  4. Introducing Natalia Ivanova (150 words): - Detail: Her brisk walk, clearly setting her apart from the relaxed gait of tourists. Her camera, worn but high-end, indicating her profession. - First Interaction: A simple "Mr. Harrington?" showcasing her direct nature.

  5. Natalia's Passion (140 words): - Backstory: A brief recount of an event she covered, showcasing the trials of the Ukrainian people. - Emotion: Thomas noting the fiery glint in her eyes — passion, pain, determination.

  6. Establishing Connection (140 words): - Dialogue: Natalia probing his true intentions; Thomas admiring her directness and countering with his genuine concerns.

  7. Thomas's Curiosity (120 words): - Body Language: Leaning in, attentive, soaking in every anecdote and piece of information. - Internal Dialogue: His realization that this is more intricate than any briefing he received.

  8. Historical Landmarks of Kyiv (140 words): - Descriptive Tour: Mentions of St. Sophia's Cathedral, Golden Gates, and Motherland Monument. Paint the resilience of structures that have stood the test of time.

  9. Through the Streets (130 words): - Interaction: Thomas trying local delicacies on Natalia's insistence, both engaging with street vendors, gathering layers of information.

  10. Contrasting Vibes (130 words): - Observation: Children laughing in the foreground; in the background, a wall graffitied with protest slogans.

  11. Descriptive Emotion (140 words): - Personal Stories: Maybe an elderly woman sharing her hope for her grandchildren's future or a young artist using art as rebellion.

  12. Thomas's Appreciation (120 words): - Realization: An internal monologue about how this isn’t just another assignment for him anymore. He's become personally invested.

  13. Parting Ways (140 words): - Body Language: A firm handshake that lingers, mutual respect evident. - Dialogue: A promise to reconvene, an acknowledgment of the weight of their shared mission.

  14. Anticipation and Intrigue (140 words): - Scene: Thomas in his hotel room, reviewing notes, perhaps finding an unexpected clue or receiving a cryptic message.

  15. Transition (130 words): - Imagery: Dawn over Kyiv, a new day with fresh challenges. - Foreshadowing: Hint at a secret meeting or a location vital to unraveling Viktor Dmytrovich’s mysteries.
