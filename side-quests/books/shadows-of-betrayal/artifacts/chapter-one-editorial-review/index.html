The revision introduces Thomas’s skills and current role before revealing his history gradually. The main editorial concerns are pacing and avoiding too much exposition at once.

  1. Introduce Thomas Harrington, a 45-year-old British diplomat with two decades of experience in intelligence, stationed in Kyiv, Ukraine. (This sets up the scene, introducing the protagonist and his setting.)
  2. Describe his daily routine, attending diplomatic functions, liaising with local officials, and subtly gathering information for the British government's intelligence agency, MI6. (This provides context about Thomas's role and responsibilities.)
  3. Highlight his proficiency in Russian, Ukrainian, and French, and his adeptness at negotiating high-stakes situations. (This demonstrates Thomas's skills and capabilities.)
  4. Show how his skills make him an invaluable asset to his team, particularly his ability to identify potential local assets and his keen understanding of the political landscape in Ukraine. (This establishes Thomas's value in his role.)
  5. Gradually reveal that Thomas is haunted by past experiences and moral dilemmas. Mention a botched operation in Chechnya and a betrayal by a former colleague, without going into explicit detail. (This sets up a subplot and adds depth to Thomas's character.)
  6. Illustrate how these past experiences affect him professionally and personally, subtly hinting at his struggle with alcoholism and strained family relationships. (This shows the impact of his past experiences on his current life.)
  7. Depict Thomas coping with these haunting memories and dilemmas. Maybe he attends therapy sessions or seeks solace in his Catholic faith. (This portrays Thomas's coping mechanisms.)
  8. Introduce a challenge that Thomas must face in his current role, such as escalating tensions or potential threats to the British embassy in Kyiv. (This introduces a plot point and raises the stakes.)
  9. Show how Thomas's past experiences influence his decision-making in this new situation, demonstrating his ethical commitment and reluctance to use violent tactics. (This displays Thomas's character traits in action.)
  10. Highlight how Thomas's skills and expertise aid him in navigating this challenge. (This further showcases Thomas's capabilities.)
  11. Conclude the scene with a cliffhanger that leaves the reader intrigued, such as Thomas receiving a cryptic message or suspecting a mole within MI6. (This creates suspense and anticipation for the next chapter.)

In this revised version, the details of Thomas's past experiences and moral dilemmas are hinted at instead of explicitly explained. This approach builds intrigue and gives the readers pieces of the puzzle to put together as the story progresses.
