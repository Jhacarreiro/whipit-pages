# A track-only brake-cooling concept

The cooling question began with a side branch from the existing air channel. That geometry mattered: a branch turning through ninety degrees is not equivalent to directing the entire incoming flow to the brake. I then considered dedicating the full intake to cooling for track use. The conversation produced a concept to investigate, not a validated duct. Airflow, brake temperatures, mounting clearance and the effect on the original channel remain unmeasured.
