Membership terms — later draft

This later proposal includes a €400 initiation fee for the annual membership. It supersedes parts of the first brief; these are historical proposals.

Download text ↓

Membership can only be obtained by referral of a current Fellow.

The vale of the full fellowship is 2.000 € a year with an initiation fee of 400 €.

There is also an Affiliate option valued at 200 € per month. Affiliates don’t have access to the Initiation process benefits or private rooms.

To become a Fellow or Affiliate one needs to complete the application process and accept the terms and conditions.

Guests Fellows can enter with up to 4 guests that can only stay on premises while the vouching Fellow is present.

20€ per guest will be debited from the vouching Fellow account upon entrance

Upon entering guests need to register and accept the terms and conditions.

Over 4 guests can be accommodated if the intention is shared with sufficient time for preparations.
