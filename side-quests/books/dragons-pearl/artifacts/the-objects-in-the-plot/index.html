SPECIAL OBJECTS:

  • The Dragon's Pearl: A legendary artifact made from the rarest form of jade, luminescent and nearly flawless. It's believed to be imbued with the spirit of the dragon, a symbol of power, wisdom, and prosperity in Chinese culture. The pearl is intricately carved with miniatures depicting pivotal moments in Chinese history, each one telling a story of its own.
  • The cryptic note: Found at the scene of the theft, written in a language that suggests the theft was premeditated and potentially involving individuals from various countries present at the banquet.

  • The Harrington Compass: An antique, ornately crafted compass once owned by a famous British explorer, given to Thomas Harrington by his grandfather. It not only holds sentimental value but is rumored to have a hidden compartment. The compass has been in the Harrington family for generations and always points true, symbolizing the steadfast nature of the Harringtons.

  • The Tsar's Cufflinks: A pair of Russian Imperial cufflinks, owned by Sergei Petrov, bearing the Romanov crest encrusted with tiny diamonds. The cufflinks were rumored to be a gift from the last Tsar to Sergei's great-grandfather, symbolizing a connection to power. Yet, unbeknownst to most, they conceal a tiny lock-picking set, a relic from Petrov's grandfather's time in the intelligence service.
