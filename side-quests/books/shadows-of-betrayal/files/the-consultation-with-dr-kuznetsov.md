# The consultation with Dr. Kuznetsov

5 - 6

From the heart of Kyiv, in the looming shadow of the Foreign Ministry, there lay the nondescript office of Dr. Ivan Kuznetsov. For many, it served as a sanctuary of sorts, a place where diplomats, politicians, and officials alike could share their burdens with the stoic psychiatrist. For Thomas Harrington, the space was a pause in the geopolitical symphony that he masterfully conducted, a moment of self-reflection that he increasingly found invaluable in his line of work.

As he ascended the final steps to Ivan's office, Thomas's mind was a whirlwind of faces and whispered words, half-formed patterns of power and shifting alliances. He appreciated these meetings, the opportunity to step back and scrutinize the tangled web of his existence from a distance.

"Ah, Thomas!" Dr. Kuznetsov greeted him as he stepped into the office, a welcoming smile on his face. The old psychiatrist was a contrast to his surroundings, a beacon of calm in a sea of tumultuous thoughts and pent-up emotions. "Do come in. How was your week?"

Thomas smiled at Ivan, taking a seat on the aged leather couch that had hosted countless souls and their shared secrets. "Good evening, Ivan. It was...quite illuminating."

"Enlightening, you say?" Ivan quirked an eyebrow, a spark of curiosity in his eyes. "Do tell."

As Thomas began to unfold the events of the week, the mosaics of diplomacy and espionage, Ivan listened with rapt attention, his keen gaze piercing through the surface. Thomas found comfort in these sessions, his words floating in the room, given weight and importance by the doctor's thoughtful presence.

"I've been gathering pieces of a puzzle," he started, his fingers subconsciously tracing the well-worn pattern on the armrest. "A speech about a military budget, whispers about 'mutually beneficial opportunities' between oil and tech giants, a close friend hinting at an upcoming infrastructure project, and-"

He hesitated for a moment, the image of a woman dressed in black invading his thoughts, her eyes behind dark glasses watching him intensely. Thomas brushed the thought aside, as he had done earlier with Marie, and continued, "There's a sense of change in the air. An anticipation, a gathering storm."

Ivan nodded, understanding gleaming in his eyes. "It's like being in the eye of the hurricane, isn't it? Everything is calm, yet you can feel the energy building, the tension rising."

Thomas agreed, "Exactly. It's a delicate dance, and I mustn't misstep."

Ivan was silent for a moment before leaning back in his chair. "Thomas, you've navigated these turbulent waters for years, and you've done it with remarkable finesse. Remember, it's alright to take a breath, to find solid ground amid the storm."

Thomas let out a quiet laugh. "Solid ground in a storm. Now there's a novel idea."

Despite the gravity of the conversation, the office was filled with the warm resonance of their shared laughter. This space was more than a psychiatrist's office for Thomas Harrington; it was a refuge, a reminder that beneath the layers of a skilled diplomat, there was a man – human, resilient, and far from alone in navigating the turbulence of geopolitical intrigue.
