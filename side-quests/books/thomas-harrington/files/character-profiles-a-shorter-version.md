# Character profiles — a shorter version

1. Thomas Harrington: A tall, lean British diplomat with sharp features and piercing blue eyes. A seasoned intelligence officer, he grapples with moral dilemmas due to his experiences in Ukraine. As he investigates, he forms a close bond with Natalia and gains a deeper appreciation of Ukrainian culture. His mentor, Marie, challenges him to confront his personal demons, facilitating his transformation.

2. Natalia Ivanova: A determined Ukrainian journalist with short dark hair. Her patriotism and love for Ukraine initially drive her, but working with Thomas leads her to re-evaluate her values. Natalia's interactions with Olena, who highlights the human cost of war, and her challenges working with Thomas shape her growth.

3. Viktor Dmytrovich: An imposing military officer with a stern demeanor. His strong sense of duty to his country underlies his actions, but the motives behind them remain murky. The unfolding investigation reveals more about Viktor, challenging his notions of patriotism and personal gain. His interactions with Thomas and Natalia lead to unexpected alliances.

4. Olena Kovalchuk: A compassionate nurse with gentle features, deeply affected by the war's human cost. Her frontline experiences drive her growth, leading her to question her values. Her interactions with Thomas provide grounded perspective on the investigation, while her relationship with Natalia reveals unexpected truths.

5. Dmitri Orlov: A charismatic operative with a quick wit and deceptive charm. A master manipulator, he uses his intelligence to stay ahead. Dmitri's interactions with Thomas and Natalia lead to unexpected alliances and revelations, and his past actions, revealed during the investigation, challenge his views on loyalty and personal gain.
