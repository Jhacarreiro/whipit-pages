Reconstructed track-session notes · 2026-06-28

When the car changed during the session

Retrospective notes from the working session. Reported observations and proposed changes are kept distinct.

Download text ↓

The June trackday added observations to the earlier upgrade plan. Grip felt worse later in the session. The change was noticeable on the first fast lap after two slower laps, which made a simple account of immediate cooling less convincing.

What was observed

Observation What the record supports
More sliding later in the session A driver-reported change in behaviour. Tyre pressure was not checked immediately on leaving the track.
Braking felt less effective A reported symptom, without a measured stopping-distance comparison.
Very different front and rear brake-temperature readings Handheld readings after a slower lap, followed by a question about whether the shiny disc surface affected the measurement.

What changed in the investigation

The conversation began with setup suggestions and moved towards the quality of the measurements. Several explanations were discussed, including tyre temperature, pressure, brake heat and regeneration. None was isolated by a controlled comparison.

Temperature-indicating paint and other ways of recording heat were then researched. The expensive paint option was rejected; the record does not confirm a later purchase or installation.

These notes add an actual driving report to the project. They leave the cause open and keep suggested settings separate from what was measured or fitted.
