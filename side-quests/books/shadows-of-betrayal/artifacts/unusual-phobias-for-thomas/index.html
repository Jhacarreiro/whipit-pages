Unusual phobias for Thomas

AI draft. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Download text ↓

  1. Ablutophobia - Fear of Bathing or Cleaning: This phobia could stem from a traumatic event during the war where the character was attacked or ambushed during a moment of vulnerability, such as bathing or cleaning equipment. This could add a unique daily challenge for the character and also have significant implications for their personal relationships.

  2. Siderodromophobia - Fear of Trains, Railroads, or Train Travel: Your character might have experienced a traumatic incident involving a train during the war - a violent ambush, a failed mission, or the loss of a comrade. In his diplomatic travels, he could be challenged by this fear, particularly if he must travel by train for certain assignments.

  3. Athazagoraphobia - Fear of Being Forgotten or Ignored: Your character, being a secret agent, lives a life of obscurity. This could foster a deep fear of being forgotten or leaving no legacy. This fear could drive your character to take risks, seeking recognition within the confines of their secret career.

  4. Hypnophobia - Fear of Sleep or Falling Asleep: This fear could be tied to recurring nightmares about the war. The fear of sleep might be affecting the character's health and professional performance. This also creates an opportunity for interesting nighttime scenes where the character engages in activities to keep sleep at bay.

  5. Dystychiphobia - Fear of Accidents: This could have developed from a tragic, accidental event in the war. In his current life as a diplomat, the character could be excessively cautious, painstakingly planning every detail of his operations and daily life to prevent accidents.

  6. Chionophobia - Fear of Snow: If the war took place in a snowy location, traumatic memories associated with it could have sparked this phobia. This could complicate missions in cold, snowy climates and could lead to evocative descriptions of how this everyday occurrence can cause fear and anxiety.
