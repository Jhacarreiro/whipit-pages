The wool trail splits in two

English translation of the Portuguese source. A separate working scene from the Rexi adaptation. The archive preserves changes in voice, props and continuity between versions.

Download text ↓

...an enormous ball of wool. Its strand, stretching mysteriously through the garden, weaves a path that awakens your curiosity. You decide to follow it, your slippers lighting every step along this puzzling trail. As you move forward, the wool winds between trees, over stones and under bushes, leading you along a route full of unexpected twists and turns. Intrigued, you continue your journey, wondering where this woollen path will take you. As you follow it, you come to two choices:

  1. The Enchanted Forest Path: The wool leads you to the edge of a dense forest, where the trees seem to whisper ancient stories. You can choose to enter the forest, exploring its mysteries and discovering its hidden secrets, guided by the wool, which seems to glow even more brightly here.

  2. The Trail to the Hidden Lake: Or you notice that the wool also branches towards a less-travelled path that seems to lead to a hidden lake. Following this trail might reveal a secret natural sanctuary, a place of peace and beauty, where the wool might have hidden its magical ending.

Each choice promises its own adventures and discoveries, leaving you to decide which path to follow, guided by curiosity and the light of your magical slippers.
