# Genre elements for the story

30 January 2024 · 15:12 · AI draft

For a junior writer crafting a story inspired by Agatha Christie within the context of space exploration, consider these genre elements:

- **Mystery**: Focus on unraveling a complex puzzle or secret, typically involving a crime or mysterious event.
- **Crime Fiction**: Integrate elements of crime, emphasizing motives, opportunities, and alibis.
- **Detective Fiction**: Feature a detective-like figure, leading the investigation and piecing together clues.
- **Whodunit**: Structure the narrative around identifying the perpetrator of a crime, often with a surprising revelation.
- **Murder Mystery**: If applicable, include a murder as the central plot point, exploring suspects and motives.
- **Psychological Thriller**: Delve into the psychological aspects of characters, exploring fears, guilt, and moral dilemmas.

Blend these elements with the futuristic and scientific aspects of space exploration to create a unique, Christie-inspired narrative.
