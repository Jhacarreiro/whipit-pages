Side Quests/Website service concept

Proposed workflow · 2026-02-24

From public information to a reviewed proposal

The roles and handoffs come from the supplied brief; they do not describe an operating service.

Download text ↓
Stage Proposed work Handoff
Discovery Find organisations with weak or missing websites using public sources. A shortlist for research.
Research Gather basic business information and review the existing online presence. A profile with the information needed for a draft.
Website preparation Choose a suitable template and prepare the content and preview. A website demo for review.
Proposal Explain the offer and optional services in plain language. A proposed package and message.
Coordination and review Check the website facts, proposal and package details. A decision to revise, discard or approve for sending.

Human involvement

The user wanted to review progress through chat while the system handled the preparation. The brief explicitly placed human review before the initial proposals were sent.

Questions left open

Which customer segment should come first? Which templates would be good enough to show? How would inaccurate business information be caught? What would the maintenance service actually include?

The surviving material records the proposed workflow and follow-up requests. It does not include a final implementation or a delivered analysis from the assistant.
