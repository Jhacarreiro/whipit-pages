# Meeting Dmitri — scene plan

1. Natalia and Thomas stand on the outskirts of Kyiv, the countryside stretching out before them. The anticipation of their meeting with Dmitri Orlov, the elusive informant, hangs heavy in the air.

2. Natalia checks her equipment, ensuring her recording devices are in working order, while Thomas checks his gun, his mind racing with thoughts of what they might uncover. The sun hangs low in the sky, casting long shadows that seem to mirror the secrets they are about to unearth.

3. Marie's voice crackles through the radio, providing last-minute instructions and reminders of the potential dangers they may face. She urges caution and emphasizes the need to stay vigilant in the presence of Dmitri, a man known for his deceitful nature.

4. As they approach the predetermined meeting spot, a secluded clearing nestled among the trees, a sense of unease settles upon Natalia and Thomas. They exchange a glance, a silent acknowledgement of the high stakes and the dangers that lie ahead.

5. Dmitri Orlov emerges from the shadows, his presence commanding attention. He exudes an air of confidence, his sharp features and piercing gaze revealing a cunning intelligence. Natalia and Thomas brace themselves for the verbal sparring that is about to unfold.

6. Dmitri begins the conversation with veiled threats and cryptic remarks, revelling in the power he holds as an informant. He plays mind games, testing Natalia and Thomas's resolve and probing for weaknesses. The atmosphere grows increasingly tense as they navigate the treacherous dance of wits.

7. Natalia employs her sharp intuition and journalistic instincts, attempting to unravel Dmitri's true intentions. She carefully chooses her words, probing for hints and seeking to expose the web of deception that surrounds him.

8. Thomas, relying on his years of experience, tries to analyze Dmitri's body language and subtle cues. He searches for cracks in the facade, hoping to gain the upper hand in this intricate game of cat and mouse.

9. Dmitri taunts and challenges them, revealing glimpses of his true nature as a ruthless operative. He dangles morsels of information, enticing them with half-truths and calculated revelations, all while maintaining an air of mystery.

10. Tension reaches its peak as Natalia and Thomas press further, their determination unwavering. They refuse to back down, determined to expose the secrets Dmitri holds and bring the traitors to justice. Dimitri holds information since he was also benefiting from the conspiracy. The intensity of the encounter leaves them breathless, their senses heightened by the weight of the moment.

11. The meeting concludes with Dmitri retreating back into the shadows, leaving Natalia and Thomas with more questions than answers. They exchange a knowing look, silently vowing to uncover the truth that Dmitri seeks to keep hidden.

12. As they retreat from the meeting spot, Natalia and Thomas reflect on their encounter with Dmitri back at the safe house. They discuss their observations and strategize their next move, aware that their investigation has taken a perilous turn. Marie's voice crackles through the communication device, providing reassurance and guidance, reminding them of the importance of their mission.

13. The chapter ends with Natalia and Thomas disappearing into the fading light of the countryside, their determination unyielding. They know that their path forward will be fraught with challenges, but they are determined to outwit the cunning Dmitri and unveil the secrets he guards so fiercely.
