# Maria Dubois — a shorter profile

Maria Dubois: A striking French intelligence operative with auburn hair and sea-green eyes, Maria is Thomas Harrington's superior and not-so-subtle admirer. Her quick-wittedness and adaptability make her invaluable in the volatile world of espionage. Despite her tough exterior, she is fiercely protective of her team. Her resilience and coolness under pressure render her an indomitable force amidst the chaos of war.
