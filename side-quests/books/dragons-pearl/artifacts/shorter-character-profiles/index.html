Thomas Harrington: A keen and self-assured British diplomat in his mid-40s with a lean build, short-cropped hair, and piercing blue eyes. Comes off as aloof, but shows a humorous, softer side to those close to him.

Maria Dubois: A resourceful French intelligence operative and mentor to Thomas, in her late 30s. She's known for her striking green eyes, sleek hair, elegant dress sense, sharp wit, and deep loyalty.

Natalia Ivanova: A fearless Ukrainian journalist in her early 30s, set on unveiling the truth. Natalia is recognizable by her curly dark hair, expressive brown eyes, bold fashion choices, and a strong moral compass despite her impulsive nature.

James Franklin: A charming and driven American diplomat in his late 40s, James is a skillful politician with a commanding presence, short-cropped gray hair, and a well-tailored suit. Beware of his persuasive charm masking a Machiavellian streak.

Li Xiang: A determined Chinese historian in her early 40s, Li is an expert in Chinese artifacts and culture. Dressed in traditional clothing, she has a sleek bob, a formal demeanor, and a passionate resolve to protect her country's heritage.

Sergei Petrov: A cunning Russian intelligence officer in his mid-50s. Sergei's silver hair, piercing blue eyes, and tailored suits contribute to his authority. His charm hides a ruthless side, making him a daunting adversary.

Dr. Elizabeth Palmer: A well-known British archaeologist in her late 40s, Dr. Palmer is a practical woman, passionate about her research on ancient artifacts. With blonde hair in a bun and no-nonsense attire, she's deeply respectful of her fellow historians.

Inspector Chen Wei: A meticulous Chinese police officer in his mid-40s, focused on solving a high-profile case. With short, black hair and a neat uniform, he exudes authority. Despite a rigid demeanor, he is deeply committed to justice and law enforcement.
