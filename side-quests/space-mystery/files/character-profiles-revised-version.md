# Character profiles — revised version

31 January 2024 · 09:30 · Supplied draft

Alex Mendoza: A brilliant scientist with a passion for knowledge and discovery, Alex is dedicated to the Mars mission's success. However, they struggle with their mental health during the journey, making it difficult for them to maintain their composure. Their psychological state leads to intense paranoia and obsessions, affecting relationships with the crew and their sense of reality.

Captain Priya Singh: A seasoned Indian astronaut, Captain Priya Singh possesses strong leadership skills and a calm demeanour. She is a natural leader who can make tough decisions under pressure. Her experience in space travel makes her a valuable asset to the team, but she must also navigate the complexities of international politics and crew dynamics.

Dr. Leo Hernandez: As an American engineer, Dr. Leo Hernandez plays a critical role in resolving technical issues that arise during the mission. Initially sceptical of Alex's behaviour, Leo's analytical mind and pragmatic approach help him remain focused on the mission at hand. His expertise is crucial to the crew's survival, but his mistrust of others may strain relationships within the team.

Elena Kozlova: Elena is a Russian communications specialist responsible for maintaining contact with Earth. She sympathizes with Alex's plight and becomes an ally, but she also hides a secret related to the mission. Her loyalty is constantly tested as she navigates her relationships with other crew members and tries to balance her duty with her personal beliefs.

Dr. Yuto Takahashi: A Japanese biologist researching the spacecraft, Dr. Yuto Takahashi provides crucial insights into the mysteries surrounding the ship. His keen observational skills and scientific curiosity make him an invaluable member of the crew. His quiet demeanour masks a deep understanding of human nature, which becomes more apparent as the story unfolds.

Dr. Nadia Buhari: As the Ghanaian medical doctor on board, Dr. Nadia Buhari is tasked with ensuring the crew's physical and mental well-being. Her pivotal role in diagnosing and treating Alex's condition puts her in a challenging position. She must balance her professional responsibilities with her personal feelings and the tense dynamics of the crew.

Zara Al-Farsi: A UAE government official and liaison for the mission, Zara Al-Farsi represents the interests of the international consortium funding the expedition. She becomes actively involved in trying to mediate conflict from aboard the spacecraft, but her allegiances are not always clear. Her ability to navigate diplomatic waters is tested as tensions rise among the crew and on Mars.

These character profiles provide a foundation for the novel, giving each character distinct backgrounds, motivations, and personalities without revealing key plot points or spoilers.
