The original club brief

Early pricing and membership proposal. Later terms in this collection differ.

Download text ↓

i have this idea about building a gentleman's club but a modern take on it. some concept/ideas I have for now:

  • obviously not just for man
  • you can only enter by being a member or entering with one
  • membership is 200€/month, if paid annually it's 2.000€ + 200€ first time. half can be spent on consumption
  • if coming with a member you pay 20€ to enter
  • members get access to a premium locker to keep their top-shelf alcohol and nothing is charged for that
  • there is no "payment moment" at the entrance everyone gives access to their PayPal to open the door and money goes from there automatically receipts by email so there is no friction on entering, leaving or paying
  • If a member pays for annual membership they get a unique experience, 4g of gold are melted right in front of them to form a unique token that they will use to enter and open their personalized locker
  • monthly we will invite artists, creatives, AI experts, etc to talk to members
  • thinking that design should be something like this: https://www.artlegacyhotels.com/
