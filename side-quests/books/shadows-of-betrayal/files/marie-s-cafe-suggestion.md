# Marie’s café suggestion

"Quite the dance, indeed," Marie responded, her voice carrying a subtle undercurrent of admiration. She lingered for a moment longer, her gaze lingering on Thomas's contemplative expression, then turned to retreat back inside the embassy.

Before disappearing through the doorway, she paused and looked back over her shoulder, a slight smile playing on her lips. "Thomas, you know," she began in a casual tone, "I've heard of a quaint little café downtown that's said to serve the best pâtisserie in all of Kyiv. Might be a good place to unwind and... discuss our work, perhaps?"

Her voice trailed off with the suggestion, her eyes meeting Thomas's for a fleeting moment. It was a suggestion wrapped delicately in the veil of a friendly invitation, but Thomas, with his seasoned instincts, understood the subtle implications.

He took a moment before responding, turning to look back at the cityscape, his mind already thinking of the layers of conversations they could unravel together, the ideas they could bounce off each other, and the insights they could possibly glean.

"That sounds like an excellent idea, Marie," he finally replied, turning back towards her with an appreciative smile. "Why don't we do that later this evening? It would be a great way to end the day."

Marie's smile widened a fraction, "Perfect. I'll see you then, Thomas." With that, she turned and disappeared into the dimly lit embassy, leaving Thomas alone on the balcony once more.

The sun had set entirely now, the vibrant orange hues replaced by a wash of indigo, speckled with stars. The city of Kyiv, steeped in secrets and whispers, lay spread out before him. Yet, in this moment of solitude, his thoughts strayed not to the intricacies of his job or the challenges of the morrow, but to the prospect of a quiet evening of shared insights and intriguing conversations, and perhaps, a delightful pâtisserie or two.
