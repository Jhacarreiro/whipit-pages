The Moscow check-up with Dr. Kuznetsov

AI draft. A rewrite that recasts the hero as Thomas Anderson in Moscow; only the first part survives. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Download text ↓

1

"Your heart rate is elevated, Mr. Anderson," Dr. Kuznetsov observed, looking at the biometric readout. He studied the data on the screen, his gaze steady and unyielding.

Thomas Anderson, an American cybercrime analyst stationed at the U.S. Embassy in Moscow, tried to maintain his calm. "I'm just not a fan of these tests, Doctor."

Dr. Kuznetsov offered a sympathetic nod. A Russian of medium height, with a stern, yet not unkind face, he was the embassy's top psychiatrist. His role was to help the embassy staff manage the stress and pressures of their demanding jobs.

"I understand," he said, his voice holding a comforting lilt. "But these check-ups are necessary. They help us understand your mental and physical state, to make sure you're fit for service."

Thomas knew the doctor was right. The work they did at the embassy was taxing, both mentally and physically. And with recent cyber threats escalating, the stakes were higher than ever.

2

Their conversation was interrupted by a knock at the door. "Come in," Dr. Kuznetsov called out.

A young woman stepped in, carrying a tray with two mugs. "The coffee you asked for, Doctor."

"Thank you, Maria," Dr. Kuznetsov said, receiving the mugs. He handed one to Thomas. "Black, just the way you like it."

Thomas accepted the mug with a grateful nod. The warmth of the mug in his hand was comforting, and the rich aroma of the coffee was calming. He took a sip, feeling the warm liquid wash down his throat, immediately feeling a little more relaxed.

"So, Thomas," Dr. Kuznetsov began, leaning back in his chair. "Tell me, how have you been feeling recently?"

Thomas pondered the question. He had always been a private person, preferring to keep his thoughts to himself. But he knew the value of these sessions. "I've been feeling... fine," he started hesitantly. "Just a bit more tired than usual."

3

Dr. Kuznetsov leaned forward, his eyes piercing. "Thomas, you've been with us for quite some time now. You're one of our most valued assets. But you're not a machine. You need to take care of yourself."

Thomas nodded, acknowledging the doctor's words. "I know, Doctor. And I am taking care of myself. It's just that with the increasing threats, it feels like there's always something more that needs to be done."

The doctor understood. "The nature of our work is such that there's always more to be done. But remember, your well-being is crucial for the work you do."

The conversation veered towards more mundane topics. They talked about the latest football match, the weather, and some of the new shows on television. But throughout the session, Dr. Kuznetsov kept a keen eye on the biometric data. There was something that wasn't quite right, and he intended to find out what it was.

As the session came to an end, Dr. Kuznetsov made a note in his file. He would monitor Thomas closely. There was more to this than met the eye, and he was determined to ensure that whatever it was, it would not interfere with Thomas' work.
