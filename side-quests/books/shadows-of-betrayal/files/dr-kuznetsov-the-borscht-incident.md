# Dr. Kuznetsov — the borscht incident

Thomas Harrington, a man whose professional life could make even the busiest of bees dizzy, found himself squirming in a less-than-comfy chair across from his psychiatrist, Dr. Ivan Kuznetsov. A bespectacled man with the sort of beard that shouted 'I read many large books', Dr. Kuznetsov's office was, to say the least, a far cry from Thomas's preferred hangout spots.

A colossal statue of Sigmund Freud stared down at Thomas from a corner of the room. It held a pipe that made it seem like it was contemplating whether to start a smoking habit. The room was filled with the sweet smell of old paper, ink, and Ivan's signature lavender-infused hand sanitizer.

"Thomas, my friend," Dr. Kuznetsov began, sitting down and brushing a few crumbs off his tweed suit jacket. "Last time we met, you seemed like a cat on a hot tin roof. How's the feline factor today?"

Thomas, after considering the question for a moment, shrugged. "Still up there, Ivan. But perhaps more of a cat on a warm tin roof now."

Ivan chuckled, a warm, hearty sound that briefly disrupted the usual stillness of the office. "That's progress, I suppose. Now, have you been keeping up with the homework I assigned last time? Remember the mindfulness techniques?"

"Ah," Thomas replied, scratching his head. "Mindfulness, yes. I've been trying to be present during my diplomat dance, as I like to call it. Even took a moment to appreciate the taste of my tea this morning instead of gulping it down in haste."

"Very good, Thomas!" Ivan said, leaning back in his chair. "Now, tell me, how are you dealing with the... you know... the borscht incident?"

Thomas let out a reluctant chuckle, recalling the unfortunate incident where he'd spilled a steaming bowl of borscht on his pants during an important dinner. "Let's just say, I've been embracing my awkwardness. I mean, what's life without a bit of beetroot stain on your trousers?"

And so, their session went on, filled with hearty chuckles, light-hearted banter, and candid conversations. From an outsider's perspective, it might have appeared like a scene from a sitcom – just a quirky British diplomat and his equally quirky Ukrainian psychiatrist. But beneath the laughter and the jesting, lay the very essence of their relationship - the patient pursuit of understanding, balance, and the universal quest for better mental health.
