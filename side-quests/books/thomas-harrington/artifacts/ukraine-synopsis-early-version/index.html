Synopsis · 2023-05-30

Ukraine synopsis — early version

User-supplied text. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Download text ↓

In the midst of the chaos and uncertainty of the invasion of Ukraine, a high-ranking military officer is found dead under suspicious circumstances. The initial assumption is that he was killed in an attack by Russian forces, but a British diplomat stationed in Kyiv, with a background in intelligence, notices inconsistencies and starts investigating the death as a murder.

As the diplomat delves deeper into the case, he uncovers evidence of foul play, suggesting that the officer was in fact murdered. The officer was known to be a brilliant strategist and was working on a secret plan that could potentially turn the tide of the war in favour of Ukraine. The motive for the murder could be tied to this secret plan, which could involve a highly strategic military manoeuvre or the revelation of a secret alliance with another country.

The investigation takes place against the backdrop of the invasion, with war conditions posing a constant threat, the restricted movement making it difficult to access certain areas or meet with key individuals, limited resources complicating forensic work, and language barriers hindering communication with locals. Political tensions complicate matters further, with various factions resisting or sabotaging the investigation.

The protagonist must navigate through these challenges while gathering clues that include unusual behaviour, physical evidence, witness accounts, and discrepancies. However, there are also red herrings laid out, including misleading evidence, false confessions or accusations, irrelevant secrets, and misinterpretation of clues.

Eventually, the diplomat manages to unravel the truth and uncovers a conspiracy or secret that the victim is about to expose, which could change the course of the war. The murderer turns out to be a high-ranking official within the Ukrainian government who is secretly working for the Russians. This official had discovered the military officer's plan and killed him to prevent its implementation, intending to pass the details of the plan to the Russians.

In a tense showdown, the diplomat confronts the official, who tries to justify their actions by arguing that they were trying to prevent further fighting and save lives. However, the diplomat counters that it's not their decision to make and that the Ukrainian people have a right to fight for their independence.

Following this confrontation, the official is arrested and the diplomat manages to recover the details of the secret plan. He passes this information on to the remaining Ukrainian leaders, who implement the plan and potentially change the course of the war.

The story takes place in various locations, including Kyiv, Lviv, Donetsk, Chernobyl, Crimea, Moscow, London, Paris, and Washington D.C. The protagonist is a suave, intelligent British diplomat with keen observation skills and a passion for justice. Other characters include Ukrainian citizens, Russian soldiers, and international reporters, each with their own distinct personalities and secrets.

The story's resolution is satisfying and inevitable, with an unexpected character revealed as the murderer and motives rooted in personal gain and patriotism. The investigation is balanced with genuine clues and red herrings, making the solution challenging but ultimately rewarding.
