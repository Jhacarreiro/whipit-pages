Synopsis — concise revision directions

31 January 2024 · 09:29·AI review

Download this document ↓

To enhance the synopsis for the junior writer, follow these straightforward and objective improvements:

  1. Character Development: Expand on characters' backgrounds, motivations, and personal growth throughout the mission.

  2. Alex’s Journey: Detail the onset and escalation of Alex's psychological issues, highlighting key events that intensify their paranoia.

  3. World-Building: Describe the technological advancements, societal changes, and living conditions in this future space exploration era.

  4. Conflict: Emphasize the stakes in the Mars territorial dispute and its impact on the crew and mission, showing how it reflects or deepens internal tensions.

  5. Pivotal Events: Outline critical moments leading to the story’s climax, like betrayals, breakthroughs, or crises, to build suspense.

  6. Ethical and Moral Questions: Address the ethical dilemmas faced by the characters, especially regarding long-term space missions and human rights.

  7. Mystery Elements: Increase the mystery around Alex’s condition and hidden mission objectives, posing questions that engage readers.

  8. Crew Dynamics: Focus on how the crew’s relationships evolve, especially in response to Alex’s deteriorating mental state and how they handle conflicts and alliances.

These points aim to provide a clearer direction for developing a narrative that's rich in character depth, thematic exploration, and engaging plot twists.
