# Balancing the centre speaker

The door speakers and tweeters in the Sprinter had already been replaced. The centre speaker was the next part of the system to rethink: its small mounting space and original connections did not match the replacement options being discussed.

## The experiment

Two small speakers were tried in place of the original centre unit. The immediate problem was balance. The centre sounded too prominent, and one attempted arrangement reduced the sound from the door speakers and side tweeters too. That observation mattered more than the first explanation of the circuit.

The working notes moved between three questions: what the original components did, how the replacement speakers behaved when connected differently, and how to reduce the centre level without losing the rest of the soundstage. The original capacitor values were never established.

## What was actually reported

By 29 June, the listening result was described as almost perfect. A small further reduction in centre volume was still being considered. The final account also corrected an assumption in the advice: the suggested capacitors had not been fitted.

This is a record of a modification and its listening result. It contains no measured frequency response, electrical verification or confirmed reconstruction of the original circuit. The useful endpoint is the reported improvement in balance; the wiring suggestions in the conversation are not reproduced as a build guide.
