Scene plan · 2023-09-06

Opening beats — a cinematic blueprint

AI draft. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Download text ↓

  1. Introducing Thomas Harrington (300 words): - Start with a cinematic approach: A close-up of a plane's wheels touching down. Cut to a pair of polished shoes stepping onto Ukrainian soil, the leather worn from countless missions. - Pan up, revealing Thomas. Give an in-depth physical description: the silver streaks in his dark hair, hinting at both age and experience; the weary yet alert gaze of his piercing blue eyes; a scar that runs along his jaw, a souvenir from a mission gone awry. - Touch upon his mental state: his cautious optimism, the weight of his responsibility, and a touch of personal nostalgia (maybe he has distant family roots in Ukraine?).

  2. Describing Kyiv (300 words): - Paint Kyiv as a juxtaposition of the old and new: Ancient churches stand proud, their golden domes reflecting the sun, while nearby modern buildings rise, representing a nation's resilience and determination. - The atmosphere is thick with the cacophony of languages, honking cars, distant laughter, and a subtle undertone of tension. Through Thomas's senses, describe the sweet aroma of local foods and the distant hum of a traditional Ukrainian song.

  3. Thomas's Initial Impressions (200 words): - Thomas finds a spot to pause, possibly a café. Here, allow readers to sit with him, taking in his sensory experience: the taste of strong Ukrainian coffee, the feeling of a local newspaper's rough texture, and the sights and sounds of the city beyond. - His inner thoughts should reflect a combination of professional alertness and genuine curiosity, drawing parallels and contrasts between this assignment and past ones.

  4. Introducing Natalia Ivanova (400 words): - Her introduction is crucial. Describe her approach: the determined stride, the camera hanging from her neck, a notebook filled with scribbles. - When their eyes meet, delve into the split second of recognition and curiosity, possibly hinting at some old stories of CIA operatives that she's covered. - Their initial conversation should be a dance of mutual assessment: Thomas noting her fiery spirit and raw honesty, while she sizes up his intentions and commitment.

  5. Highlighting Natalia's Passion (250 words): - Let her guide Thomas to a memorial, a place significant to the city's recent struggles. Here, amidst the silent stone tributes, she shares heart-wrenching tales she's covered: children caught in crossfire, lovers separated by war, and the constant Ukrainian spirit that refuses to wane. - Thomas, though trained to stay emotionally distant, finds himself moved. This moment is a silent pledge from him to the city and its stories.

  6. Establishing Connection through Dialogue (200 words): - Their walk and talk should explore cultural nuances. Maybe Thomas attempts (and humorously fails) to pronounce a local dish. They could share anecdotes: Natalia's experiences during her journalistic pursuits and Thomas recalling a humorous incident from a previous assignment. - The dialogue should be a mix of light-hearted banter and profound reflections, laying the foundation for their growing bond.

  7. Thomas's Curiosity (250 words): - As they walk through Maidan Nezalezhnosti (Independence Square), Thomas's trained eyes pick up subtleties: a wall scribbled with protest slogans, a mother clutching her child a bit too tightly, young men with eyes that have seen too much. - Natalia, observing him, appreciates his depth and shares more localized insights, further deepening his understanding.

  8. Describing Historical Landmarks (250 words): - The duo visits sites like the Kyiv Pechersk Lavra and the Motherland Monument. Natalia, playing the part of a tour guide, peppers in historical facts with personal memories. - They might encounter local artisans, children playing traditional games, or elderly residents sharing tales, further immersing Thomas in the cultural richness.

  9. Walking through Kyiv (250 words): - Delve into the colors, sounds, and smells. The golden hues of the sunset, the melodic chime of church bells, the tantalizing scent of grilled meats from a nearby stall. - This walk should be a sensory journey for both Thomas and the reader.

  10. Highlighting Contrasts (250 words): - A stop at a local marketplace shows the vibrancy of daily life. Yet, in the crowd, there's a man with a military posture, eyes scanning constantly — a soldier who can't leave the war behind. - Natalia's narration and Thomas's observations should jointly highlight the city's spirit and scars.

  11. End with Strengthened Bonds (250 words): - As the day wanes, they stand on a bridge overlooking the Dnieper River. The city lights shimmer, reflecting both the city's beauty and the duo's blossoming partnership. - They part with a promise of meeting the next day, the weight of their mission a silent specter between them.
