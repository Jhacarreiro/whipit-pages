Scene plan · 2023-05-30

Chapters one to three — a step guide

User-supplied text. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Chapter 1

Thomas Harrington, a seasoned British diplomat with a background in intelligence, is stationed in Kyiv, Ukraine. He leads a predictable life, attending diplomatic functions and gathering information for the British government. He is skilled in intelligence gathering, multilingual, and adept at negotiating high-stakes situations. However, past experiences and moral dilemmas continue to haunt him.

Chapter 2

The invasion of Ukraine disrupts Thomas's life. His superior, Marie Duval, tasks him with investigating the suspicious death of Viktor Dmytrovich, a high-ranking military officer. Thomas learns that Viktor was working on a plan that could shift the war's balance in Ukraine's favour. This plan involves revealing a secret alliance with another country. He grapples with the decision to accept this mission, fully aware of the danger and moral implications it entails.

Chapter 3 step guide

  1. Introduce Thomas, a British diplomat with a background in intelligence, who is offered a mission to go to a war-torn country.
  2. Describe Thomas' initial reaction to the mission, including his fear of the risks involved and the guilt of meddling in a conflict that isn't his, driven by his passion for justice.
  3. Show Thomas struggling with his decision and weighing the pros and cons of accepting the mission, highlighting the moral complexities of war and the question of what justice means in a time of conflict.
  4. Introduce Marie, a high-ranking official within the British intelligence community and Thomas's mentor, who prompts him to reconsider his decision.
  5. Show Marie expressing her own thoughts and feelings about the mission and its importance, serving as a guiding force for Thomas throughout his journey.
  6. Have Marie listen to Thomas' concerns and fears, and offer her own perspective on the situation, highlighting the potential risks and rewards of accepting the mission.
  7. Show Thomas beginning to see the mission in a new light, thanks to Marie's words, including the potential positive impact he could have on the people affected by the conflict.
  8. Have Thomas ask Marie more questions about the mission and the people involved, highlighting his growing sense of responsibility to help those in need.
  9. Show Thomas becoming more and more convinced that he should accept the mission, including his desire to make a positive impact and help those in need, driven by his passion for justice.
  10. Have Thomas make his final decision to accept the mission, and explain his reasoning, highlighting the moral dilemmas he faced and his sense of purpose and responsibility to help those in need.
  11. Show Marie's reaction to Thomas' decision, and how she supports him, including her admiration for his bravery and willingness to help others.
  12. End the scene with Thomas feeling more confident and determined to complete the mission, highlighting the core theme of the story and the moral complexities of war.
