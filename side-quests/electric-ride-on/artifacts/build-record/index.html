Built. Photos to follow.

The electric ride-on upgrade was completed. This collection keeps the working material behind it while photographs and the final hardware description are added.

The September 2025 discussions looked at motors, reduction gearing, wheel size and ways to get more from a small electric vehicle. They also considered whether a different donor chassis would make a better starting point.

What the record establishes

  • A physical upgrade was carried out, confirmed by the builder.
  • The planning discussion included drivetrain sizing, component choices and chassis alternatives.
  • Photographs will be added separately.

The final motor, battery, gearing and chassis combination has not yet been documented here. The different ideas in the notebook should not be read as a list of parts all fitted to the same vehicle.
