# A space mystery — first concept

1. **Setting**: The novel could be set in a near-future world where space exploration has become the new frontier for geopolitical and corporate competition. The backdrop might include a major multinational space agency or a private space exploration company.

2. **Central Mystery**: The story could revolve around a mysterious incident on a moon base or a spacecraft bound for the Moon, given the heightened interest in lunar exploration mentioned in the 2023 events. This incident could be a mysterious disappearance, an unexplained malfunction, or a sabotage.

3. **Diverse Cast of Characters**: Reflecting the global nature of space exploration, the cast could include astronauts, scientists, engineers, and government officials from various countries. These characters could represent the different interests and tensions between nations, as seen in the geopolitical shifts of 2023.

4. **Subplots Reflecting 2023 Events**:
    - A subplot could involve diplomatic tensions reminiscent of the Nagorno-Karabakh conflict, perhaps involving territorial disputes on the Moon or Mars.
    - Another subplot could delve into the demographic shifts and economic challenges faced by countries like India and China, mirrored in the story by the shifting balance of power in space exploration.

5. **Themes of Espionage and Political Intrigue**: Given the potential for the militarization of space and the lack of established space governance, the novel could feature espionage and political intrigue, with nations and corporations engaging in covert operations to gain an advantage in space.

6. **Technological and Ethical Questions**: The story could explore the ethical implications of space exploration, such as the exploitation of extraterrestrial resources, the creation of space debris, and the risks of human space travel.

7. **Climactic Resolution**: True to Christie's style, the resolution would involve a surprising twist, revealing the truth behind the central mystery. This could be tied to broader themes of human ambition, the perils of unchecked technological advancement, and the need for international cooperation in space exploration.

The novel would not only be a thrilling mystery but also a reflection on the complexities and challenges of our era's most ambitious endeavors. Christie's ability to weave intricate plots with profound themes would make this a compelling and thought-provoking read.
