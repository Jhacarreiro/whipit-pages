# Character profiles — the longer July version

1. Thomas Harrington: A tall and lean British diplomat with sharp features and piercing blue eyes. He is a seasoned intelligence officer, but his experiences in Ukraine push him to confront his values and morals. He begins to question the decisions he's made in the past, leading to a transformative character growth arc. As he navigates the complexities of the investigation, he develops a close bond with Natalia and gains a deeper understanding of Ukrainian culture. His growth is also shaped by his interactions with Marie, who serves as a mentor figure and challenges him to confront his personal demons.

2. Natalia Ivanova: A striking Ukrainian journalist with short dark hair and a fierce determination. She is deeply patriotic and driven by her love for Ukraine, but her experiences working with Thomas lead her to question her own values and beliefs. She develops a close friendship with Thomas, but their partnership is not without its challenges. Natalia's growth is shaped by her interactions with Olena, who provides a unique perspective on the human cost of war. Her journalistic instincts and patriotism are tested throughout the investigation, leading to a transformative character growth arc.

3. Viktor Dmytrovich: A burly and imposing military officer with a steely gaze and a stern demeanor. He is dedicated to his country and has a strong sense of duty, but his motivations are not always clear. As the investigation unfolds, Viktor's past and present actions are revealed, leading to a complex character growth arc that challenges his views on patriotism and personal gain. His interactions with Thomas and Natalia shape his growth, leading to unexpected alliances and personal revelations.

4. Olena Kovalchuk: A kind and compassionate nurse with soft features and a gentle voice. She is deeply affected by the human cost of war, and her experiences shape her interactions with Thomas and Natalia. Olena's growth is driven by her experiences on the front lines of the conflict, leading her to question her own values and beliefs. Her interactions with Thomas provide a grounding perspective on the investigation, while her relationship with Natalia leads to unexpected personal revelations.

5. Dmitri Orlov: A handsome and charming operative with a devious smile and a quick wit. He is a master manipulator, using his intelligence and charisma to stay one step ahead of those seeking to uncover his secrets. Dmitri's growth is shaped by his interactions with Thomas and Natalia, leading to an unexpected alliance and personal revelations. His past and present actions are revealed throughout the investigation, leading to a complex character growth arc that challenges his views on loyalty and personal gain.
