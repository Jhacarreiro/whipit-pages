# Chapter five — the full draft

1 - 2

Natalia headed to Kyev, determined to continue using her knowledge of the country and its people to make a difference. Her love for her homeland made it impossible for her to abandon its people now.

At about the same time Natalia was entring Kyev city limits Maria and Thomas reached the edge of battle-ravaged Ukraine. An intelligence operative with many years of experience, Maria moved with calm assurance, while both Thomas Harrington and Natalia Ivanova, had their eyes fixed ahead. Their destination - Crimea - was clear: they were there to use their combined skills to collect essential data. The devastating impact of war weighed heavily on them both.

"Stay vigilant," Maria whispered, her voice barely audible over the howling wind. "We're entering enemy territory."

The landscape around them was bleak and unforgiving. Buildings lay in ruin, their once-proud facades now nothing more than rubble and dust. Bullet casings littered the ground, a haunting reminder of battles fought and lives lost. Sniper fire could be heard echoing in the distance, the sharp cracks adding to the symphony of destruction.

Thomas nodded solemnly, his thoughts racing. Every step he took carried the risk of walking into a sniper's crosshairs or stepping on a landmine hidden beneath the debris. He couldn't help but feel a sense of moral ambiguity; his mission demanded success at any cost, but the people caught in the crossfire were innocent civilians.

"Maria," Thomas said, trying to keep his voice steady. "What's our plan for avoiding these threats?"

"Stick close to me," she replied, her eyes scanning the environment. "I know this area well. We'll avoid the main roads and use what's left of the buildings as cover. And remember, trust no one. Hostile locals may have sided with the enemy."

As they ventured deeper into the ravaged city of Sevastopol, it became increasingly apparent that danger lurked behind every corner. A sudden explosion rocked the ground beneath their feet, sending a cloud of smoke and debris billowing into the air. Thomas instinctively crouched low, his heart pounding in his chest.

"Are you alright?" Maria asked, concern etched on her face.

Thomas nodded, taking a deep breath to steady his nerves. "Yes, just a little shaken."

Maria offered him a reassuring smile before continuing onward. They navigated the treacherous terrain with caution, aware that one misstep could be their last. The weight of their mission pressed down upon them like an invisible burden, a constant reminder of the human cost of war.

In the midst of this desolate landscape, Thomas couldn't help but admire Maria's unwavering determination. It was clear she had been forged in the fires of conflict, her resolve as unyielding as steel. He knew he would need to rely on her expertise and trust in her instincts if they were to succeed in their mission.

As darkness enveloped the city, casting shadows that seemed to dance and taunt them, Thomas couldn't shake the sense of unease that settled in the pit of his stomach. The moral ambiguity of war hung over them like a dark cloud, a storm waiting to unleash its fury. And yet, there was no turning back now. The fate of countless lives rested in their hands, and they were determined to see the mission through – whatever the cost.

3 - 4

Thomas glanced at Maria as they cautiously trekked through the ruins of Sevastopol. The mission that had dragged them here weighed heavily on his mind. It felt like a lifetime ago when they were assigned to investigate the death of a Ukraine military officer, Viktor Dmytrovich. Now, they had ventured deep into a war zone in an attempt to make it to a Russian safe house that MI6 had identified but never actually visited. Their aim was to uncover more information about this so-called Arquitect and discover what plot he was behind to extend the conflict for financial gain.

"Maria," Thomas whispered, "I can't help but feel like we're being watched."

"Stick to the plan, Thomas," she replied, her voice steady and confident. "Trust your instincts, but also trust in our training."

As they moved forward, Thomas couldn't shake the feeling that every step brought them closer to danger. In his role as an intelligence operative, he'd faced numerous challenges, but this mission seemed different—more perilous and morally complex. He knew that by identifying these militants, he could potentially save innocent lives, but at what cost to himself and those around him?

"Checkpoint ahead," Maria warned, her tone tense.

Thomas squinted into the distance and saw the outline of a military checkpoint, heavily guarded and illuminated by unforgiving floodlights. They would have to pass through it in order to continue their mission, but doing so carried considerable risk. If caught, they could be tortured for information or killed on the spot.

"Any ideas?" Thomas asked, his heart racing.

"Let me handle this," Maria replied, her eyes scanning the scene before them. "Just follow my lead and stay close."

They approached the checkpoint, trying to blend in with the sparse foot traffic. As they neared the guards, Thomas felt sweat trickle down his back, his nerves fraying with each passing second.

"Identification," barked one of the guards, his rifle slung across his chest.

"Of course," Maria said smoothly, producing documents from her pocket. She handed them over, her expression calm and composed.

Thomas held his breath as the guard scrutinized their papers. He mentally rehearsed the cover story they'd been given, preparing himself to answer any questions that might arise.

The guard's steely gaze scrutinized every edge of the documents, tracing each letter as if searching for any possible deception. Sweat beaded on Thomas' forehead, his heart thundering in his chest as he watched the guard's fingers curl around the butt of his rifle. A few probing questions later, and they were waved through the checkpoint. Thomas exhaled a shaky breath, feeling like he'd just passed through the gates of hell and back. He glanced at Maria beside him, her face calm and collected despite the danger that had just passed. An overwhelming wave of gratitude washed over him – her quick thinking and cool demeanor had saved their lives once again.

As they continued on their way, Thomas couldn't help but admire Maria's poise under pressure. She was an invaluable partner, and he knew that without her, this mission would likely have ended in failure.

"Good work back there," he whispered, his voice filled with gratitude.

"Thanks," she said. "But remember, we're not out of the woods yet."

5 - 6

As Thomas and Maria slipped away from the checkpoint, they rounded a corner into a narrow, dimly lit alley. Their footsteps echoed off the crumbling walls of the buildings that flanked them, abandoned remnants of a bygone era. The air was thick with tension, and Thomas couldn't shake the feeling that they were being watched.

"Stay alert," Maria whispered, her eyes darting back and forth as they moved deeper into the desolate space.

The MI6 database had indicated an area surrounded by dilapidated apartment complexes where frequent persons of interest met, they didn't know of the specific location or building since intel was only provided by satelittes in the last few years.

As they kept walking Thomas scanned the shadows, he noticed a group of people lurking in the darkness. They were dressed in shabby clothes, their faces hidden by hoods and scarves. They moved with an air of purpose, but something about them struck Thomas as off—there was a subtle menace that seemed to emanate from them.

"Do you think they're connected to the Arquitect?" he whispered, his eyes never leaving the shadowy figures.

"I wouldn't rule it out," Maria said grimly. "It's best if we keep our distance."

Thomas nodded in agreement, not wanting to risk their mission by engaging with these shady-looking individuals. However, just as they began to inch away from the alleyway, one of the figures stepped forward and called out to them.

"You two! What are you looking at?!" The voice boomed through the alleyway, commanding and all-encompassing.

Marie whispered "Thomas, I need you to use your language skills here," she continued, nodding towards a small group of locals huddled together in the shadows.

"Understood," Thomas replied, his heart pounding in his chest as he approached the group. He cleared his throat and began speaking in fluent Russian.

"Excuse me, we're looking for help," he said tentatively, hoping to gain their trust. The locals looked at him warily, their expressions guarded.

"What do you want?" one man asked gruffly, his eyes narrowing as he sized up Thomas and Maria.

Thomas looked at the group of locals, their suspicious eyes fixed upon him. He needed to come up with a convincing excuse, something that would appeal to their interests and make them more willing to assist.

"We're journalists," Thomas replied, his voice calm but authoritative. "We're investigating the situation here in Ukraine, trying to uncover the truth about what's really happening. We believe there's a conspiracy that's prolonging the war and causing unnecessary suffering."

The locals exchanged glances, their curiosity piqued by the mention of a conspiracy. They had likely seen the devastating effects of the war firsthand and might be sympathetic to the idea of exposing those responsible.

"We need your help," Thomas continued, his voice filled with urgency. "We've come across some crucial information, but we need someone who knows the area well to guide us and ensure our safety. We believe you have valuable knowledge that could shed light on the truth."

The man hesitated and seemed to study Thomas for a moment, his gaze unwavering, glancing around nervously before finally relenting. With a nod, he motioned for them to follow. It seemed their words had struck a chord, and the locals were willing to assist them in their investigation.

Thomas exchanged a quick glance with Maria, relief washing over him. The locals' willingness to help could prove to be a significant breakthrough in their mission. With their newfound allies, they could gather more information, uncover leads, and expose the conspiracy that plagued Ukraine.

As they followed the locals into the depths of the alleyway, Thomas couldn't help but feel a glimmer of hope. Perhaps, with their combined efforts, they would inch closer to the truth and bring justice to those responsible for the war's prolonged suffering.

Thomas and Maria followed the locals deeper into a alleyway, their hearts pounding with a mix of anticipation and trepidation. The man leading them finally stopped at a corner, his gaze fixed on a specific building.

"There it is," he said, his voice hushed. "That's the place. But I must warn you, no one crosses that street. It's dangerous."

Thomas and Maria exchanged glances, their curiosity piqued. They were eager to investigate further, but the warning weighed heavily on their minds. They knew they couldn't underestimate the risks involved.

"What's in that building?" Thomas asked, his voice steady despite the underlying unease.

Their guide eyes narrowed, a flicker of hesitation crossing his face. After a momentary pause, he spoke in a low, ominous tone, "Since the start of the war, those two big luxury Mercedes SUVs parked in front of that building come and go. It's best if you stay away if you value your lives."

Thomas and Maria absorbed the information, their minds racing with possibilities. Could they have found the correct building so easily? All this intrigued them, fueling their determination to uncover the truth.

"Thank you for the warning," Maria said, her voice firm. "We appreciate your concern."

"Thank you for your help," Thomas said gratefully to the locals. "We'll take it from here. Please stay safe."

With a nod of understanding, the group melted back into the shadows, leaving Thomas and Maria standing at the corner. Their eyes focused on the building that held so many mysteries.

Thomas and Maria absorbed the information, their minds racing with possibilities. The presence of luxury vehicles and important figures intrigued them, fueling their determination to uncover the truth.

As they contemplated their next move, a chilling silence enveloped the air. Suddenly, a group of masked men emerged from the adjacent buildings, their presence imposing and threatening. The tension in the air became palpable, causing a knot to form in the pit of Thomas' stomach.

"Go away," one of the masked men growled, his voice laced with menace. "This is not a place for you. Leave now, or suffer the consequences."

Thomas and Maria exchanged a quick, apprehensive glance. It was clear that the masked men were determined to keep them away from the building, whatever secrets it held.

Just as Thomas and Maria were about to make a desperate escape, a third party intervened, distracting the masked men and allowing them to make their escape. Two women dressed in local outfits appeared from nowhere wielding wooden rods in their hands. They shouted at the masked men fiercely and then proceeded to swing their rods. The distraction was enough for Thomas and Maria to take advantage of the altercation and flee the scene.

They ran through a labyrinth of alleyways, not daring to look back until they reached the safety of an abandoned building.

As they stood there, panting with exhaustion, they looked behind them in awe at what had just transpired.

7 - 8

The sun dipped below the horizon, casting long shadows across the scarred landscape. Maria and Thomas trudged through the debris-littered streets of a small town, their footsteps muffled by the thick layer of dust that coated everything in sight. They kept a cautious distance from one another, eyes constantly scanning for signs of danger.

"Maria, we need to find shelter for the night," Thomas said, his voice strained. "I don't think I can go much further without some rest."

"Over there," Maria replied, pointing to an abandoned building with boarded-up windows. "It's not much, but it should keep us safe for the night."

As they approached the structure, they noticed a group of locals huddled around a makeshift fire, their faces etched with lines of weariness and fear. Maria locked eyes with a woman cradling a baby in her arms, a look of determination and defiance shining through her exhaustion.

"Evening," Maria greeted the group, her voice warm yet firm. "We mean you no harm. We're simply looking for a place to rest for the night."

"Join us," the woman with the baby offered, gesturing to the fire. "But know that we cannot protect you from any dangers lurking in the night."

"Thank you," Maria responded, accepting the invitation. She settled by the fire, her posture relaxed yet alert.

Thomas watched Maria interact with the locals, noting how she seemed to effortlessly gain their trust. She exuded a sense of calm and confidence that put others at ease, even in this desperate environment. It was clear that Maria's loyalty to her agency was unwavering, but her willingness to take risks and connect with those they encountered revealed a deeper understanding of the human cost of their mission.

"Maria," Thomas whispered, pulling her aside. "We shouldn't stay too long. Our presence puts them at risk."

"Agreed," she replied. "But if we want to succeed in our mission, we need their help. They know this area better than anyone."

As the evening wore on, Thomas felt the physical toll of their journey catching up to him. His limbs ached from hours of walking, and his shoulders burned from the weight of his backpack. He had seen the horrors of war firsthand, the destruction it wrought on both the land and its people. The fatigue gnawed at the edges of his consciousness, but he pushed it aside, knowing that they couldn't afford any mistakes.

"Thomas," Maria said softly, her eyes searching his face. "You look exhausted. Try to get some sleep. I'll keep first watch."

"Thank you, Maria," he replied gratefully, nodding off almost as soon as his head hit the ground.

Maria watched over Thomas and the group of locals, her senses attuned to any potential threats. The night was eerily quiet, the silence punctuated only by the distant echoes of gunfire and the occasional cry of a lone animal.

"Tomorrow, we continue our search for the truth," Maria thought, her resolve unwavering. "No matter the cost."

9 - 10

As the sun began to rise, it cast its light upon a scene of ruins. It wasn't a coincidence that they chose this building: it was situated close to an intersection containing the primary roads that connected the city to both the harbor and Simferopol. These routes provided the most discreet paths to come in and out of town.

"Maria," Thomas said, as he quickly sat up, dawning realization etching its way across his face. "The SUVs!" he exclaimed. "They're heading to the port."

Without another word, the two scrambled outside, their footsteps muffled by the thick layer of dust that coated everything in sight. The sun was just beginning to peak over the horizon as they watched the convoy move in the direction of the port. They were too far away to make out who was inside, but it didn't matter- they had to act quickly if they wanted to follow them.

Maria and Thomas sprinted through the ruins of what used to be a thriving city, leaping over rubble and broken glass with ease. They kept a careful watch for signs of trouble, their nerves and reflexes on high alert. As they approached the port, they noticed that there seemed to be no security presence- either it was a false alarm or they were walking right into a trap.

They quickly ducked behind a shipping container and surveyed the area. The SUVs were parked in front of a large warehouse, and from the looks of it, no one was guarding them. They could make out two figures in one of the vehicles, but with the darkness still clinging to them they couldn't tell who it was.

Thomas pulled out his binoculars and focused on the scene. As he did, his heart jumped into his throat- sitting inside the SUV was none other than Jared Beckett himself! A wave of shock gripped him as Thomas tried to process what he had just seen.

Jared Beckett was among the wealthiest people on the planet, with investments in a wide range of industries from ecommerce to space exploration and defense...

Maria motioned towards a stack of crates that would provide them with some cover while they figured out their next move. Taking her lead, they both crept towards their hiding spot, ducking behind it just as two men exited the warehouse and began approaching the car.

After taking a better look it seemed Jared Beckett and another man were in the car, accompanied by two bodyguards standing outside. Suddenly, a small boat appeared from around the corner and pulled up alongside the SUV. The bodyguards opened the doors and hustled Jared Beckett and his companion onto it before quickly pushing off. As they disappeared into the darkness of the early morning light, Maria and Thomas knew that their search for answers had come to an end- but they still had no idea what was in store for them next...
