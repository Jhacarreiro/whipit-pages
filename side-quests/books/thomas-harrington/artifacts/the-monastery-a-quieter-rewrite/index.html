The monastery — a quieter rewrite

AI draft. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Download text ↓

In the dim light of St. Michael's Golden-Domed Monastery, Thomas Harrington found himself, quite ironically, kneeling before the flickering candles. He was as out of place in this sanctum as a shark in a kiddy pool, his head lowered not in prayer, but in deep contemplation.

"I really should've been a circus performer," he muttered under his breath. His words, a mix of wistful regret and self-deprecating humor, resonated within the church's stoic silence, absorbed by the indifferent stone walls. Ah, the joys of introspection in a house of worship he didn't subscribe to!

"Thomas?" A gentle voice jolted him out of his musings. Spinning around like a startled cat, he saw Father Petrov standing in the corner, the man's face etched with concern.

"Father, didn't see you lurking in the shadows," Thomas blurted, springing to his feet like a jack-in-the-box.

"Your mumblings were... intriguing," Father Petrov remarked, his tone lightly laced with curiosity. "What's on your mind?"

Thomas huffed out a laugh. "Just thinking about how different life could've been if I'd pursued a career in juggling or tightrope walking. Less perilous than my current line of work, you know?" He dodged mentioning his past, the tormenting memories he'd come here to grapple with. Somehow, turning it into a metaphorical circus felt fitting, an amusing spin to his complicated existence.

Before the Father could respond, Thomas' phone buzzed in his pocket, its piercing tone shattering the cathedral's tranquility. Apologizing with a sheepish grin, he glanced at the text message. His heart leaped to his throat.

“I need to run, Father,” Thomas said, his words spilling out in an excited flurry. “The circus calls!”

As he dashed towards the door, he could almost hear the symbolic lions roaring. What a strange world he found himself in, where kneeling in a church stirred up less peace than the chaos waiting for him outside.
