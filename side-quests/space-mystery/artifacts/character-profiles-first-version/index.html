Characters

Character profiles — first version

31 January 2024 · 09:25·Draft for review

Download this document ↓

Alex Sterling: A brilliant scientist with an enigmatic personality, Alex is deeply passionate about their work in space exploration. As the central figure of the story, they harbor a strong determination to uncover hidden truths. Physically, Alex has short-cropped hair and sharp facial features that hint at their calculative nature. Their psychological struggles are apparent as they grapple with the stress of the Mars mission.

Captain Priya Singh: An experienced astronaut from India, Captain Singh is known for her calm demeanor and strong leadership skills. She is tall and slender, with a commanding presence that inspires respect from the crew. Her eyes are warm but focused, reflecting her unwavering dedication to the mission.

Dr. Leo Hernandez: An American engineer with a pragmatic outlook, Dr. Hernandez plays a key role in troubleshooting technical issues aboard the spacecraft. He is initially skeptical of Alex's behavior and prefers to rely on logic and reason. Physically, he has a muscular build and a scruffy beard that contrasts with his meticulous approach to his work.

Elena Kozlova: A Russian communications specialist responsible for maintaining contact with Earth, Elena is empathetic towards Alex's plight but also harbors her own secrets. She has a lithe frame and long, wavy hair that frames her face. Her eyes convey both compassion and intelligence, making her an intriguing presence on the ship.

Dr. Yuto Takahashi: A Japanese biologist conducting essential research on the spacecraft, Dr. Takahashi provides critical insights into the mysteries aboard the ship. He is a slender man with glasses perched atop his nose, giving him an air of quiet authority. Despite his reserved nature, his dedication to his work speaks volumes about his character.

Dr. Nadia Buhari: A Ghanaian medical doctor tasked with ensuring the crew's physical and mental well-being, Dr. Buhari is pivotal in diagnosing and treating Alex's condition. She is a tall, statuesque woman with a warm and nurturing presence, exuding an air of confidence and competence that the crew relies on.

Zara Al-Farsi: A UAE government official and liaison for the mission, Zara represents the interests of the international consortium funding the mission. She is actively involved in trying to mediate conflicts from aboard the spacecraft, showcasing her diplomatic skills. Physically, she is petite with a strong and determined gaze, reflecting her tenacity and commitment to her role.

These characters form the core ensemble of the novel, each with their unique personalities and roles in the story. Their interactions and relationships drive the narrative, creating the psychological tension that underpins the novel's exploration of humanity's struggles in the face of a new frontier.
