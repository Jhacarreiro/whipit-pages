First character profiles — review

31 January 2024 · 09:26·AI review

Download this document ↓

Revision priorities for the initial character profiles: personal motivations, relationships and connections between professional roles and the central mystery.

  1. Alex Sterling: The detailed description of Alex sets a strong foundation for a complex protagonist whose mental and emotional journey could be the heart of the narrative. To deepen Alex's character, consider exploring the specific origins of their passion for space and how their past experiences shape their reactions to the mission's challenges.

  2. Captain Priya Singh: Captain Singh's leadership qualities and physical description suggest a character who's both respected and approachable. Further exploring her leadership style, how she balances the pressures of command with personal interactions with the crew, and her own vulnerabilities could add layers to her character.

  3. Dr. Leo Hernandez: Hernandez's skepticism and logical approach make him a grounding force within the story. It might be interesting to delve into what drives his pragmatism—is it a personal history, a specific event, or his inherent nature? Also, exploring how his skepticism challenges or supports the crew could create compelling dynamics.

  4. Elena Kozlova: Elena's empathy and secretive nature set her up as a character with potentially conflicting loyalties or hidden agendas. Fleshing out her backstory, what secrets she's hiding, and how these secrets affect her role on the mission could make her role more pivotal.

  5. Dr. Yuto Takahashi: Dr. Takahashi's quiet authority and dedication are intriguing. To expand on his character, consider what specific research he's conducting and how it ties into the mission's goals or the unfolding mystery. His personal motivations for joining the mission could also enrich his character.

  6. Dr. Nadia Buhari: As the caretaker of the crew's well-being, Dr. Buhari's role is crucial. Exploring her medical philosophy, how she copes with the pressures of keeping the crew healthy under extraordinary conditions, and her interactions with Alex can highlight her importance beyond her medical duties.

  7. Zara Al-Farsi: Zara's role as a mediator and government liaison suggests a character skilled in negotiation and diplomacy. Delving into her motivations for taking on such a challenging role, her perspectives on the mission, and how she navigates the complexities of space politics could provide depth.

The next revision should connect character backgrounds, personal goals and fears to the central plot, then track how relationships and responses change during the mission.
