# The monastery — another variation

Under the dim, shadowy light of St. Michael's Golden-Domed Monastery, there knelt Thomas Harrington, a sight as incongruous as a penguin in the Sahara. Before the flickering candles, his head bent down, not in an act of devotion but in a token gesture of respect for the sanctity of the place.

Bowing to his past rather than a divine entity, Thomas muttered under his breath. "If there's any universal force, deity, alien overlord, or sentient teapot out there," he murmured, "just give me a heads-up when the next catastrophe is coming. Oh, and while you're at it, a bit of courage to face my past wouldn't go amiss either."

No sooner had he finished his half-mocking, half-serious invocation than a sharp, squeaking sound pierced the silence of the cathedral. Startled, Thomas sprang to his feet, his eyes scanning the gloom for the source of the noise. What he saw made his mouth drop open in sheer disbelief.

From the corner of the cathedral, a small mouse had emerged, dragging behind it a piece of cheese as big as itself. Apparently, the tiny rodent had mistaken the silence of the cathedral for safety and decided to embark on its own daring mission.

"Blessed cheese, anyone?" Thomas couldn't help but chuckle at the irony. Maybe this was the sign he'd asked for. Maybe he just needed to take a page out of the mouse's book - be bold, daring, and always remember to save some cheese for later. Life was, after all, a strange blend of courage, confrontations, and... cheese. He left the cathedral with a spring in his step, ready to face whatever life was going to throw at him - hopefully, something a little less stinky than cheese.
