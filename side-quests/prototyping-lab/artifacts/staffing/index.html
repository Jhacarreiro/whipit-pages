Historical estimate · 2025-05-12

Four roles for an initial team

English translation of the Portuguese source. A 2025 concept draft. Estimates, currencies, totals and assumptions are preserved as written, not reconciled or checked against supplier quotes.

Download text ↓

Below is an estimate of annual gross salaries for the four roles in a laboratory in Portugal, based on market averages and adjusted so that technicians and support staff are not paid at the minimum:

Role Annual gross salary (€) Comments
Laboratory coordinator 30 000 Management, safety and machine workflow
Mechanical fabrication technician 22 000 CNC, woodworking, maintenance and calibration
Electronics and assembly technician 22 000 Electronics bench, testing and small repairs
IT/CAD support 25 000 Licences, workstations, networking and CAD/CAM
Total annual payroll 99 000

Notes on these figures

  • Coordinator: assumes a degree and experience managing technical laboratories.
  • Technicians: pay above the minimum, reflecting specialist skills in operating and maintaining prototyping equipment.
  • IT/CAD support: includes software management (SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Altium, etc.), workstation maintenance and user support.

This budget puts payroll just below €100k per year, allowing the facility to attract qualified staff and operate safely and efficiently.
