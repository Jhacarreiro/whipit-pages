# The original club brief

Supplied brief · 2024-02-27

Early pricing and membership proposal. Later terms in this collection differ.

---

A contemporary members’ club concept, open to all genders.

- Entry is restricted to members and their guests.
- Membership is proposed at €200 per month, or €2,000 annually plus a €200 initial fee. Half is available for consumption.
- Guests entering with a member pay €20.
- Members receive a premium locker for their spirits, at no additional charge.
- PayPal-linked access and automatic emailed receipts remove a separate payment step at entry or departure.
- Annual membership includes an experience in which 4 g of gold is melted into a unique token for entry and access to the member’s personalized locker.
- Monthly events feature artists, creatives, AI experts and other speakers.
- Design reference: https://www.artlegacyhotels.com/
