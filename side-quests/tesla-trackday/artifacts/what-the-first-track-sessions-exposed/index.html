A July track-session report

Retrospective notes from the working session. Reported observations and proposed changes are kept distinct.

Download text ↓

The track report separated two limits: braking consistency after sustained laps, and the loss of regenerative braking over consecutive laps. I reported brake fade after roughly ten minutes and reduced regeneration after three or four laps. These are driving observations, not instrumented measurements. Brake fluid, hoses and airflow were discussed as next areas to investigate; the report does not establish which change solved the problem.
