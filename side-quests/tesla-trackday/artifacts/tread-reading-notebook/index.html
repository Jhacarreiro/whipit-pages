Reconstructed measurement notebook · 2026-07-01

Separate existing wear from wear during the test

Retrospective notes from the working session. Reported observations and proposed changes are kept distinct.

Download text ↓

A later alignment discussion included before-and-after tread readings for all four tyres. That made it possible to ask two different questions: which edge was already more worn, and which edge lost more tread during the latest interval.

Read the change, not just the final number

The front tyres still had less tread on the inner side after the test. However, the figures recorded for that interval showed a larger reduction on the outer side. Those statements can both be true. Confusing the accumulated wear with the recent change had made the earlier discussion difficult to follow.

Part of the record Treatment in the notebook
Front readings Preserve the before-and-after pairs rather than infer the latest wear from the final depth alone.
Rear-right inner reading Flag the inconsistency: the reported depth increased between measurements.
Suggested replacement for that reading Keep it as a hypothetical correction, separate from the original observation.
Next alignment setting Record it as a proposed test, not a confirmed completed adjustment.

What the figures do not establish

The readings were described as approximate, and the driving interval included track use. The record cannot isolate alignment from driving conditions, measurement position or other variables. It also cannot support a reliable road-life projection.

The next test was selected at the end of the discussion. The useful addition to the project is the measurement discipline: retain the original numbers, identify the doubtful reading, and distinguish a proposed test from a result.
