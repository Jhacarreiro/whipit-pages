The ransacked office

AI draft. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Download text ↓

"The stakes are getting higher, Natalia," Thomas whispered back, taking the newspaper clipping and studying it. "We need to be careful."

Just then, a loud crash echoed through the café, and the lights flickered ominously. The patrons began to murmur nervously as the waitstaff rushed to the source of the noise. A cat, startled by a toppling stack of plates, darted out from the back room, its tail fluffed up in alarm.

"But sometimes being careful is not enough," Natalia said, her gaze fixed on something or someone across the room.

Following her gaze, Thomas spotted a man in a dark coat leaving the café hurriedly. The man looked over his shoulder, his eyes briefly meeting Thomas's before disappearing into the night.

"That man...," Thomas began, but Natalia cut him off.

"He's been following us. I noticed him a few days ago," she confessed, her knuckles white as she gripped the newspaper clipping.

Outside, the man - Dmitri Orlov - pulled out a cell phone, dialing a number. "It's time to escalate things," he said into the phone, a wicked grin on his face as he disappeared into the darkness.

The following morning, as Thomas and Natalia arrived at their makeshift office within the British embassy, they were met with a chilling sight. Their office had been ransacked, papers strewn about, furniture overturned, and their carefully compiled research missing.

On the wall, scrawled in dark red paint, was a threatening message: "Stop digging, or you'll be buried."

Natalia looked at Thomas, her face pale but her eyes fierce. "This won't stop us," she declared, her voice echoing in the ravaged room. "We won't let it."

Outside, hidden in the shadows, Dmitri Orlov watched, his plan set in motion. His next move would surely drive the fear deeper, pushing them further on the edge. The game, after all, was only just beginning.
