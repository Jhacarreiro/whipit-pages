Marie’s invitation

AI draft. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Download text ↓

"Last night was eventful," Thomas said, his gaze fixed on the cityscape.

Marie watched him for a moment, a subtle intrigue in her eyes. "I heard about a café downtown," she said lightly, the suggestion hanging in the air. "Serves the best pâtisserie in Kyiv. We could go... unwind a bit and talk more?"

Thomas turned towards her, recognizing the invitation for more than just a casual meet. "Sounds like a plan, Marie. Let's do that," he said, a hint of anticipation in his voice.

"Perfect. See you then, Thomas," Marie smiled, disappearing into the embassy. As the Kyiv night unfolded before him, Thomas found himself looking forward to their evening of shared conversation and sweet treats.
