# Thomas reveals his role

"Thomas, we need to talk," Natalia said as they began the laborious task of picking up the pieces of their ruined investigation.

"Agreed," he replied, a seriousness in his tone that gave Natalia pause. She had come to know Thomas as a steadfast ally, dedicated to their shared cause, but there was a gravity to his demeanor that seemed new. 

"What is it?" Natalia asked, anxiety creeping into her voice. 

Thomas sighed heavily, running a hand through his hair. He seemed to be weighing his words carefully, an internal struggle playing out behind his eyes. Finally, he spoke. 

"Natalia, I recognized the man from last night."

She looked at him, puzzled. "Who is he?"

He paused before answering, as if gathering his thoughts. "His name is Dmitri Orlov. He's... not someone you want to mess with."

Natalia's brow furrowed. "How do you know him?"

He looked at her for a moment, an unspoken apology in his eyes. "Because my job isn't exactly what I led you to believe. I'm not just a diplomat. I work for MI6."

Natalia blinked in surprise. She had suspected that Thomas wasn't entirely forthcoming about his role at the embassy, but she had never expected this.

"You're a spy?" She asked, trying to hide the shock in her voice. 

"In a manner of speaking," Thomas replied. "I gather intelligence, analyze threats, prevent potential crises. And Orlov, well, he's been on our radar for some time."

"But why didn't you tell me?" Natalia demanded, her eyes flashing with betrayal.

Thomas winced at the hurt in her voice. "Because it was safer for you not to know. But now...," he sighed. "Now, we're in this together."

"And what do we know about this Orlov?" Natalia asked, her journalistic instincts kicking in.

"He's a Russian operative," Thomas explained. "He's been involved in numerous shady operations, including this illegal logging business we've been investigating. Orlov is ruthless and will stop at nothing to get what he wants. Which is why we need to be extra careful moving forward."

Natalia nodded, taking in the weight of Thomas's revelation. She felt a rush of mixed emotions, but the predominant one was a strengthened resolve.

"Thank you for telling me, Thomas," she said, meeting his gaze. "We're in this together, and we will expose Orlov's crimes."

Thomas gave her a small smile, a glimmer of relief visible in his eyes. "I'm glad you understand, Natalia. But remember, we're not only up against Orlov. We're challenging an entire network of corruption. It's going to get a lot more dangerous from here."

"As long as we stick together, we'll be okay," Natalia replied, her voice filled with unwavering conviction. "Let's clean this place up. We have work to do."

Outside, under the grey Kyiv sky, Dmitri Orlov watched the British embassy, his cold gaze fixated on the window of the ransacked office. His game had taken an unexpected turn, but he was far from finished. They had shown their hand, now it was his move.
