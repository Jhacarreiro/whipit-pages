Side Quests/Books/The Dragon’s Pearl

Scene plan · 2023-07-06

Chapter one — original beats

User-supplied text. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Download text ↓
  1. Introduce Thomas Harrington, a seasoned British diplomat attending an international diplomatic event at the American embassy in Beijing.
  2. Set the scene and establish the attendees, including Thomas's colleagues Maria Dubois, Natalia Ivanova, James Franklin, and Li Xiang, a Chinese intelligence officer.
  3. Establish the purpose of the event, which is to discuss the theft of a valuable Chinese artifact, the Dragon's Pearl.
  4. Show Thomas's interest in the Dragon's Pearl and his intrigue about its history and current whereabouts.
  5. Describe the Dragon's Pearl in detail, highlighting its beauty and significance.
  6. Establish the importance of the Dragon's Pearl to the case that will follow, as it is believed to be the key to solving the theft and preventing a diplomatic crisis.
  7. Show Thomas interacting with his colleagues, discussing the case and the Dragon's Pearl, as they try to build alliances and gather information.
  8. Highlight the tension and potential conflict between the different nationalities and interests represented at the event.
  9. Show Thomas navigating the complex diplomatic landscape, trying to build alliances and gather information, as he tries to find a way to recover the stolen artifact without causing a diplomatic incident.
  10. End the scene with a sense of anticipation and tension, as the case and the fate of the Dragon's Pearl hang in the balance, and Thomas and his colleagues are left wondering what will happen next.
