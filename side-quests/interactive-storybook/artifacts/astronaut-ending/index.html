An ending with the astronaut

English translation of the Portuguese source. A separate working scene from the Rexi adaptation. The archive preserves changes in voice, props and continuity between versions.

Download text ↓

Discovering the little toy astronaut inspires Rexi to imagine himself as a space explorer, turning every pedal stroke into a journey through the universe. With the astronaut as his co-pilot, he feels that every lap of the garden is a giant leap in the exploration of new planets and galaxies. The combination of his bicycle and glowing slippers makes the night-time adventure even more magical, a symbol of the endless discoveries waiting among the stars.
