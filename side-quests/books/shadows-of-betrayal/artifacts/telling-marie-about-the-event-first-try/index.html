Telling Marie about the event — first try

AI draft. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Download text ↓

"Ah, the event," Thomas said, looking down at his empty teacup. "It was... informative."

Marie raised a perfectly arched eyebrow. "Informative? You have piqued my curiosity, Monsieur Harrington."

Thomas laughed, setting his teacup down on the balcony railing. "Well, it was certainly a gathering of Kyiv's finest. All the movers and shakers, as they say."

He paused, considering how much to divulge. It was always a balancing act - maintaining the trust of his colleagues while also safeguarding the information he was privy to. Marie, though she was trustworthy, did not need to know everything.

"Anything of importance we should be aware of?" Marie asked, her curiosity clear in her eyes.

"Perhaps," Thomas responded, keeping his voice neutral. "Trade agreements and cultural exchanges were the talk of the evening, but there were undertones of... something else. I can't quite put my finger on it yet."

Marie looked thoughtful. "That's very vague, Thomas. Even for you."

Thomas chuckled again, leaning on the balcony railing. "Vague, indeed. But then, that's the nature of this game, isn't it?"

Marie nodded, her gaze drifting out towards the city. "Yes, it certainly is. Anyway, I'm sure you'll decipher it in due time. You always do."

He nodded, his mind already racing ahead to the next piece of the puzzle. There was something stirring in Kyiv, something that sent a frisson of anticipation down his spine. Whatever it was, Thomas Harrington would be ready to face it.

As Marie left him on the balcony, he picked up his cup and gazed at the slowly darkening city. The game was afoot, and he was eager to see what the next move would be. In the game of international diplomacy, information was king, and Thomas was its master player.

And with that, he turned from the balcony and headed inside. Tomorrow was another day, filled with new information to gather and interpret, more dots to connect. With a last glance at the city that was slowly becoming a second home, he retired for the night, the thoughts of the day still swirling in his head.
