# An astronaut in the sandpit

Scene draft · 2024-03-15

English translation of the Portuguese source. A separate working scene from the Rexi adaptation. The archive preserves changes in voice, props and continuity between versions.

---

As his skilful hands kept digging and shaping the sand, he felt something hard beneath the surface. Curious, he brushed the sand aside, revealing a small toy astronaut whose suit glowed softly in the sunlight. The sight of the astronaut, lost in the vastness of the sandpit, brought a smile to his face, mixed with a little nostalgia for the stories of space exploration he loved so much.

The astronaut, with its small silver visor and detailed suit, seemed ready to set off on a mission into the unknown, a symbol of the adventures he longed to experience himself. Holding the toy gently, he suddenly felt inspired to return to his own mission of discovery.

Remembering the strand of wool he had begun following before losing himself in the fun of the sandpit, he put the astronaut in his pocket as a travelling companion and looked around for the wool. There it was, tracing a curious path through the garden, inviting him to continue the adventure.

With a new friend in his pocket and the promise of discoveries ahead, he left the sandpit behind, now a distant planet of sand. Following the wool with renewed enthusiasm, he was ready to see where this space journey would take him, guided by starlight and the courage of an explorer of the unknown.
