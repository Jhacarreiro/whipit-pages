# An ending with the dragon

Scene draft · 2024-03-15

English translation of the Portuguese source. A separate working scene from the Rexi adaptation. The archive preserves changes in voice, props and continuity between versions.

---

Finding the dragon on his adventure transports Rexi into a world of fantasy and courage. This dragon, now perched on his bicycle's handlebars, becomes a magical guardian on his night-time rides. Together, they face imaginary dragons and save enchanted kingdoms, while the glowing footprints from his slippers guide them through epic battles in the shadows of the night. Having the dragon beside him fills Rexi with unshakeable courage, turning every pedal stroke into a legendary adventure beneath the stars.
