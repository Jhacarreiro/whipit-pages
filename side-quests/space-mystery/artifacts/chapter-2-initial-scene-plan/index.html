Chapter 2 — initial scene plan

31 January 2024 · 14:28·Draft for review

Download this document ↓

  1. Start the chapter with Alex's increasing paranoia becoming evident through their actions and thoughts. Show them constantly looking over their shoulder, jumping at small noises, and exhibiting signs of anxiety.

  2. Introduce the first strange occurrence on the spacecraft, such as flickering lights or unexplained noises. Describe it in a way that creates an eerie atmosphere and leaves the reader questioning its origin.

  3. Show Alex's growing obsession with the strange occurrence, as they start to document and investigate it. Have them take notes, record audio or video, and search for any patterns or explanations.

  4. Create a conversation between Alex and Elena Kozlova, where Alex confides in her about their concerns regarding the strange occurrences. Show Alex's trust in Elena and their belief that she might understand or have insights into the situation.

  5. Use Elena's response to validate Alex's suspicions and hint at a hidden agenda related to the territorial dispute over Mars' Elysium Sector. Have Elena share her own observations or experiences that support Alex's theories.

  6. Introduce another strange occurrence, different from the first one, to further heighten the suspense. Make it more intense or unsettling than the previous occurrence, leaving the reader even more intrigued and concerned.

  7. Show Alex's increasing determination to uncover the truth behind these occurrences and their connection to the Elysium Sector dispute. Have them become more proactive in their investigation, seeking out clues and potential allies.

  8. Create a moment of doubt for Alex, where they question their own sanity and wonder if their paranoia is unfounded. Use internal monologue or dialogue with Elena to convey this internal struggle.

  9. Reveal a small piece of evidence or information that strengthens Alex's belief in the hidden agenda. It could be a cryptic message, a suspicious conversation overheard, or a hidden document that they stumble upon.

  10. Increase the tension by having Alex and Elena encounter an obstacle or setback in their investigation. It could be a crew member catching them in the act or a sudden interruption that forces them to pause their search temporarily.

  11. Show Alex's determination to overcome the obstacle and continue their investigation, despite the risks involved. Have them devise a plan or strategy to gather more evidence or confront the source of the strange occurrences.

  12. End the chapter with a cliffhanger or revelation that leaves the reader eager to find out more. It could be a shocking discovery, a confrontation with a suspicious character, or a realization that the hidden agenda runs deeper than they initially thought.
