The idea

An Agatha Christie mystery, sent to Mars.

On the way to Mars, the crew's most brilliant scientist starts to unravel: paranoia, hallucinations and the conviction that the mission is hiding something. Below them, two blocs claim the same region of Mars, and the crew is sent into the middle of it.

The twist spoiler

One of the planned endings reveals that the mission never left the ground. It is a VR simulation, built to test how a crew holds up under psychological and political pressure, and one of the crew is an undercover agent.
