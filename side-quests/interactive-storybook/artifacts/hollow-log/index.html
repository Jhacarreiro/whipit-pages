Two choices at the hollow log

English translation of the Portuguese source. A separate working scene from the Rexi adaptation. The archive preserves changes in voice, props and continuity between versions.

Download text ↓

ahead of you lies a hollow log, bathed in shadow, hiding secrets in its inviting darkness. But with your courage and the slippers lighting your way, you feel ready to face the unknown. As you approach this new adventure, two choices appear:

  1. Explore the inside of the hollow log: Taking a deep breath, you decide to investigate what is hiding in the darkness. Who knows what mysteries or creatures might be waiting in this natural hiding place?

  2. Go around the log and follow the wool: Preferring not to disturb whatever might be hidden in the shadows, you choose to follow the strand of wool, curious to discover where it leads, leaving the mystery of the log behind.

Each choice will take you along a different path through this adventure, full of discoveries and surprises. Which path will you choose?
