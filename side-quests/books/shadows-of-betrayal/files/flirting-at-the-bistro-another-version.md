# Flirting at the bistro — another version

Chapter 5: The Unspoken Waltz

At "Помаранчевий Нектар," the night unfurled with soft murmurs and shimmering lights. Nestled in their private corner, Thomas and Marie found themselves lost in a labyrinth of stories, each tale punctuated by warm laughter and softened by the slow sips of velvety cocktails. As minutes ebbed into hours, a delicate, almost ethereal connection started to unravel between them.

Throughout their conversation, the tango of Marie's intellect and Thomas' captivating mystery was hard to ignore. It gave birth to an electric current of intrigue that hovered between them. As they weaved their pasts into a tapestry of conversation, the undercurrents of mutual attraction gently tugged at them.

Marie's soft chuckle dissipated into the jazz melody playing in the background, a light dusting of mirth lingering in her eyes. "Thomas, you are full of surprises," she said, tracing the rim of her cocktail glass with a graceful finger. 

Her comment drew a soft chuckle from Thomas. "Well, they do say that mystery adds an element of charm, don't they?"

"Perhaps," she replied, her eyes shimmering with quiet amusement. "But it also makes you wonder what one might be hiding beneath the layers."

"Does it?" Thomas quipped, a teasing grin tugging at his lips. "And what makes you think I'm hiding something?"

"Isn't it the nature of our line of work, Thomas?" Marie retorted playfully. "Diplomacy is a masquerade, after all."

As the banter danced back and forth, the atmosphere around them was suffused with an unspoken understanding. The lingering gazes and half-veiled smiles were testament to a flirtatious undercurrent that neither chose to vocalize. It was a tantalizing dance of words and silence, an exquisite anticipation that made the evening sparkle with possibility.

With every anecdote Thomas shared, the enigma surrounding him seemed to deepen, drawing Marie further into his world. In return, every question Marie posed, every smile she offered, felt like an invitation, a path leading Thomas towards a captivating labyrinth he found hard to resist.

They both found comfort in this intricate dance, the unspoken words filling the air with an intoxicating warmth. Their exchange, while seemingly innocent, was an exquisite orchestration of subtly flirtatious gestures. 

As the evening waned and the night flirted with the dawn, a silent consensus passed between them. The connection they had kindled was not merely one of diplomatic camaraderie but a dance that promised intriguing depths yet to be explored. They parted ways with a shared glance, a promise that hung in the night air, the thrilling anticipation of their next meeting subtly hinted but left unsaid.
