Scene plan · 2023-05-30

Chapters one to three — a revised step guide

AI draft. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Download text ↓

Chapter 1: Shadows of the Past

  1. Introduce Thomas Harrington, a seasoned British diplomat stationed in Kyiv, Ukraine.
  2. Highlight Thomas's expertise in intelligence gathering, multilingualism, and negotiating high-stakes situations.
  3. Delve into Thomas's inner struggles, haunted by past experiences and moral dilemmas.
  4. Showcase his desire for redemption and the search for solace amidst the chaos that surrounds him.

Chapter 2: The Call to Action

  1. Introduce the invasion of Ukraine, disrupting Thomas's predictable life.
  2. Present Marie Duval, Thomas's superior, who tasks him with investigating the suspicious death of Viktor Dmytrovich.
  3. Reveal the shocking revelation that Viktor was involved in a plan that could shift the war's balance in Ukraine's favor.
  4. Emphasize Thomas's internal conflict as he weighs the risks, moral implications, and the question of accepting the mission.

Chapter 3: Natalia's Resolve

  1. Shift focus to Natalia Ivanova, a resourceful and enigmatic Ukrainian journalist.
  2. Explore Natalia's strong sense of justice, love for her country, and her role as a skilled journalist.
  3. Highlight Natalia's unexpected alliance with Thomas and Marie, forming an unlikely partnership driven by their shared mission.
  4. Emphasize Natalia's perspective as a local and her determination to expose the truth and protect her country.
