Leaving the castle for the afternoon snack

English translation of the Portuguese source. A separate working scene from the Rexi adaptation. The archive preserves changes in voice, props and continuity between versions.

Download text ↓

Suddenly, your parents' voices bring you back to reality, announcing that your afternoon snack is ready. With a smile, you realise that your adventure in the castle and the imaginary banquet were only a prelude to the real feast waiting for you.

Before you tuck in, though, there is the task of putting away the castle of cushions and sheets. You turn the tidying into one last adventure, travelling through the kingdom in your magical slippers and gathering the pieces of the castle with care and affection. Every cushion is a memory of battles won; every sheet, a flag of triumph. With your parents' help, the castle is carefully taken apart, with promises of new buildings and adventures to come.

Leaving your imaginary kingdom behind, you follow your parents into the kitchen, where a table laden with your favourite foods awaits. Between bites and laughter, you share the stories of your adventures inside the castle with your family, enjoying not just the food but also the warmth and love that turn every meal into a celebration.

And so, with a full stomach and a happy heart, you are already planning your next great adventure. You know that gathering around the table to eat and share stories is one of the most magical moments of all, and that every adventure, even one that needs tidying up afterwards, lays the foundations for the next journey of imagination.
