# A darker prayer

"Lord, grant me the strength to face the darkness within me, the courage to confront the monsters that I've created," he muttered, his voice barely a whisper. His words seeped into the profound silence of the church, absorbed by the ancient stone walls around him.

Suddenly, a cold gust of wind blew through the cavernous space, extinguishing the row of flickering candles. An unnerving darkness descended, seemingly swallowing his plea, the tranquillity of the church abruptly replaced by an unsettling eeriness.

His heart pounded in his chest, a palpable echo of a past life, a past he wished he could bury deep within the recesses of his memory. It was a haunting reminder of a gruesome period he could never truly escape. The sense of dread was as unmistakable as it was overwhelming, a cold shiver trailing down his spine, as if the very demons he pleaded for the strength to confront had decided to answer his call.
