# Marie’s mentoring chapter — a scene plan

1. The scene opens with Natalia and Thomas seated at a small table in a dimly lit room, their faces etched with determination. The air is thick with anticipation as they discuss the mission ahead, their voices hushed to avoid unwanted attention. The weight of the task at hand is palpable, and their shared commitment to uncovering the truth hangs in the air.

2. Marie enters the room, her presence commanding attention. She joins Natalia and Thomas at the table, her gaze steady and unwavering. Sensing the hesitation in Thomas's eyes, Marie begins to share her own experiences and struggles during times of crisis. She recounts a poignant moment when she faced a harrowing decision between saving her team or completing a crucial mission, her voice laden with the weight of past sacrifices.

3. As Marie shares her story, her words carry a sense of wisdom and authority. She implores Thomas to consider the importance of their current task and the potential consequences of failure. Her conviction and the gravity of her words leave an indelible impact on Thomas, stirring his own sense of duty and the urgency to act.

4. Thomas initially hesitates, his doubts and insecurities laid bare. He expresses concerns about his own abilities and the risks involved, grappling with the fear, guilt, and helplessness that gnaw at him. Marie listens attentively, understanding the weight of his reservations, yet steadfast in her belief in his capabilities.

5. With a gentle yet resolute tone, Marie helps Thomas recognize the significance of his unique skill set. She highlights his expertise in cryptography and his exceptional ability to analyze complex data. She paints a vivid picture of his indispensable role in unraveling the truth about Viktor's death and the mysterious plan, appealing to his intellect and reminding him of the invaluable contribution he can make.

6. Thomas's eyes widen as he begins to grasp the magnitude of his skills and their potential impact on the investigation. He asks probing questions, seeking further guidance from Marie, his curiosity piqued by the allure of the truth that lies just beyond their reach. The seeds of confidence begin to take root within him, nurtured by Marie's words.

7. Through precise and detailed dialogue, Marie outlines a clear path for Thomas to follow, imparting specific instructions on how to approach the investigation and which tools to employ. Her guidance serves as a compass, pointing Thomas in the right direction and providing him with the necessary tools to navigate the treacherous terrain of their mission.

8. As Marie speaks, Thomas nods in agreement, his determination growing with each passing word. He takes diligent notes, capturing the essence of Marie's guidance, and his previously wavering resolve transforms into a steely determination to unearth the truth. The weight of responsibility settles on his shoulders, and he is ready to embrace the challenges that lie ahead.

9. The room is suffused with a dim light, casting long shadows across the worn wooden table. Papers rustle under the weight of their importance, and the aroma of freshly brewed coffee lingers in the air, mingling with the scent of anticipation. The characters lean forward, their body language mirroring their unwavering focus, their eyes shining with determination.
