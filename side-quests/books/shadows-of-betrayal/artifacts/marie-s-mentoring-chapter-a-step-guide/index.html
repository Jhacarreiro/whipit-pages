Marie’s mentoring chapter — a step guide

The previous chapter ends with Natalia and Thomas about to face someone in the shadows of the Kyiv countryside, while Marie waits at a safe house as backup.

Chapter focus: "Marie shares her own experiences and struggles in making difficult decisions during crisis times, convincing Thomas to accept the mission. She helps him understand the importance of his unique skill set in uncovering the truth about Viktor's death and the mysterious plan."

  1. Begin the scene with Natalia and Thomas finding sitting at a table in a dimly lit room, discussing the mission. (Driver: Search for truth, Guardrail: Avoid spoilers)
  2. Have Marie share her own experiences and struggles with making difficult decisions during crisis times, specifically mentioning a time when she had to choose between saving her team or completing the mission. (Driver: Search for truth, Focus: Marie's leadership and mentorship qualities)
  3. Use Marie's personal struggles to convince Thomas to accept the mission, emphasizing the importance of their task and the potential consequences of failure. (Driver: Search for truth, Character motivation: Marie wants justice to be served and the plan to be implemented)
  4. Show Thomas initially hesitant to accept the mission, expressing doubts about his abilities and the risks involved. (Driver: Challenges of investigation, Character motivation: Thomas feels fear, guilt, and helplessness)
  5. Have Marie help Thomas understand the importance of his unique skill set in uncovering the truth about Viktor's death and the mysterious plan, specifically mentioning his expertise in cryptography and his ability to analyze complex data. (Driver: Search for truth, Focus: Marie's conversation with Thomas)
  6. Show Thomas beginning to see the significance of his skills, asking more questions and expressing interest in the mission. (Driver: Search for truth, Character motivation: Thomas wants to uncover the truth)
  7. Use dialogue to convey Marie's guidance to Thomas, giving specific instructions on how to approach the investigation and what tools to use. (Focus: Marie's conversation with Thomas)
  8. Show Thomas becoming more confident in his abilities, nodding in agreement and taking notes. (Driver: Challenges of investigation)
  9. Use descriptive language to create a vivid picture of the scene, describing the room, the lighting, and the characters' body language. (Guardrail: Avoid spoilers)
  10. Use sensory details to engage the reader's senses, describing the smell of coffee, the sound of papers rustling, and the taste of a stale donut. (Guardrail: Avoid spoilers)
  11. Show the emotional impact of Marie's words on Thomas, describing his facial expressions and body language as he listens to her. (Driver: Challenges of investigation)
  12. End the scene with Thomas accepting the mission and feeling motivated to uncover the truth, shaking Marie's hand and thanking her for her guidance. (Driver: Search for truth, Character motivation: Thomas wants to uncover the truth)
