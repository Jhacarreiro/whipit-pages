# The next morning — a fragment

7 - 8

The next morning, Thomas was in his office early, sifting through a mountain of files, searching for potential leads. The chatter of diplomats and administrative staff in the background was a dull hum compared to the whirlwind of thoughts racing in his mind. His conversation with Marie the previous night was still fresh, his past failures and regrets lingering heavily.

Just as he was trying to refocus his thoughts on the current mission, a soft knock on the door interrupted him. It was Natasha, his assistant, holding a sealed envelope. "This just arrived for you, sir," she announced, placing the envelope on the corner of his desk before excusing herself.
