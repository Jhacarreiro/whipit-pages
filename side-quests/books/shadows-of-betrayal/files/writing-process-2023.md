# From premise to scene beats

Shadows of Betrayal · Writing process · 2023

Process brief reconstructed from the author’s 2023 writing notes. It consolidates the planning method; it is not a conversation transcript.

## The starting point

The notes use Agatha Christie’s mystery fiction as a reference and place the proposed novel against the invasion of Ukraine. The planning questions connect a central crime with the course of the war, the investigation and its resolution.

## Build the mystery

Set out the protagonist’s journey, the places visited and the challenges encountered. Decide how the crime affects the wider conflict, how the mystery is resolved, and which clues, red herrings and themes carry the story.

## Develop the cast

Expand each character through physical description, personality, motives and past experience. Track psychological strengths and weaknesses, relationships, possible growth and the events that could change them.

## Keep a story reference

Collect characters, settings and special objects as separate reference material. Make those details specific enough to support an outline and the scenes that follow.

## Turn the outline into scenes

Specify what happens, where it happens and who or what is involved. Revisit the outline to raise the stakes and the sense of tension, then break it into detailed scene beats that give a prose draft enough direction.

## Revise in passes

The notes alternate expansion, compression and feedback: explore an idea in more detail, shorten it when needed, and review it before continuing. They describe a working method rather than a finished manuscript.
