# The log-throwing contest — a more intense telling

"Sit back, Marie," I started, the edges of my mouth quirking up in a half-smile as I recalled that unforgettable day. "Let me transport you to Bosnia, an unlikely setting for a tale of light amidst the grim shadows of duty."

We had stumbled upon a village teeming with life, immersed in a peculiar competition, a log-throwing contest. The air was thick with anticipation and excitement; the villagers, brimming with enthusiasm, were hurling logs at makeshift targets. It was a sight to behold, the raw energy, the rustic charm - it was like being thrust into the heart of a vibrant pastoral painting.

"Can you picture us?" I asked, a chuckle escaping my lips. "A band of stern-faced soldiers, suddenly thrust into the centre of this boisterous spectacle. The language barrier was there, but the language of joy and camaraderie is universal, isn't it?"

I paused, allowing the memory to wash over me. "And the part that never fails to amuse me is that we, yes, we, emerged as one of the winning teams. An accolade that none of us had ever imagined!"

As our time in the village drew to a close, we were bid farewell with a warmth that was overwhelming. The villagers, their faces radiant under the twinkling stars, waved us off. The sound of their laughter and the sight of their joy-filled eyes followed us as we drove away. The memory of it was like a warm ember, glowing amidst the cold starkness of our duty.

"That memory, Marie," I concluded, "it's etched into my soul. Amidst the grim reality of our mission, we found a moment of pure, unadulterated joy. It's a tale that stays with me, a beacon of light in the darkest of times."

I leaned back, my eyes gleaming with the vividness of the memory. "A strange pairing, wouldn't you agree? The harshness of war and a rustic log-throwing contest. Yet, it's these moments, Marie, these unexpected bursts of colour, that breathe life into the otherwise stark canvas of our lives."
