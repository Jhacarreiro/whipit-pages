The log-throwing contest — told by Thomas

AI draft. Surviving writing draft; alternative versions are retained.

Download text ↓

So, there I was, smack-dab in the middle of Bosnia, right? It was supposed to be another routine mission, a walk in the park. But, you see, fate had a different plan.

Out of nowhere, we stumble upon this quaint little village, all abuzz. It felt like the entire place was alive, pulsating with energy and laughter. And they were having this bonkers competition, right?

It was a log-throwing contest, of all things! Can you believe it? There we were, a bunch of hardened soldiers, not knowing a word of their language, suddenly drafted into this riot of a local celebration.

With every log that sailed through the air, the crowd went wild. It was contagious, I tell you, we were laughing, clapping, all caught up in the fervor. I mean, who knew log-throwing could be such a hoot?

And the best part? We actually won! Or, well, tied for first. Not exactly the accolade I imagined earning in my military career, but hey, a win is a win, right?

As we were leaving, the villagers waved us off, their faces glowing under the starry night, still chuckling at the spectacle we'd all been a part of. I tell you, Marie, it was surreal. In the midst of all the chaos, amidst the harsh realities of war, we'd found a moment of pure, unadulterated joy.

And as our vehicle disappeared into the night, the echoes of their laughter still ringing in my ears, I knew this was one for the books. A memory to be revisited in times of hardship, a reminder of the whimsicality of life. Who'd have thought, eh? War and log-throwing contests, quite the combination!
