# A different present: glowing footprints

Scene draft · 2024-03-14

English translation of the Portuguese source. A separate working scene from the Rexi adaptation. The archive preserves changes in voice, props and continuity between versions.

---

For his birthday, Rexi received a pair of slippers that left footprints glowing in the dark. Every step lit the way with shimmering dinosaur footprints, turning his run around the garden into a magical adventure beneath the stars.
